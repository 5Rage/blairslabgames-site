    Getting started

    Your first ten minutes in the workshop, step by step, and what the game reveals as you go.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. The first steps
    2. What unlocks as you play
    3. Meet your guide
    4. Your first Fold
    5. While you are away

    You start straight in the workshop, and the opening prologue plays after your first few catches. A short hands-on tutorial in a separate practice workshop teaches the basics, with optional lessons for the advanced parts. You can replay the guide any time from Settings.

    The first steps

    1. Catch your first Moments. Tap the small clock in the centre of the board, or hold the CATCH A MOMENT button to repeat. A well-timed tap earns more. Your starting Splitter also produces automatically, so you never have to keep tapping.
    2. Upgrade your Splitter. Tap the Splitter on the inner ring. Its controls open at the bottom of the screen. Choose x1, x10, x25 or Max levels, check the price, then tap Upgrade. More levels mean more production.
    3. Make room. Tap the locked socket next to your Splitter and buy it with Moments. Core sockets are permanent, and they survive every Fold. Then tap an empty socket and install another machine.
    4. Connect your machines. Tap a machine, choose Route from here, and tap another. Arrows show the direction. See rings and routes.
    5. Watch the load. Above the clock you will see Load and a limit. Stay at or under the limit and every machine runs at full speed. See load and stability.

    What unlocks as you play

    Screens unlock as you progress, and the game points out each new one as it opens:

    • At the start you see only the Workshop, with the catch button and your Splitter.
    • Soon after, you can unlock more sockets. A first message waits in City and Journal.
    • Next, routes unlock, and Discoveries appear under More.
    • Then Research and machine modifications open. After two discoveries, try the Efficient modification.
    • Later, expanded rings, saved layouts and the Layouts screen become available.
    • Finally the Fold screen previews your first Fold, with a target of 2.5 million Moments earned in a run. Spending Moments does not reduce that total.

    Meet your guide

    Characters give you short goals, like catching ten Moments by hand, raising a Splitter to level 10 or running two machines. Each finished goal can be claimed for a reward worth about two minutes of your current production, so it stays useful at every stage. See characters.

    Your first Fold

    When you reach 2.5 million Moments earned in a single run, the Fold screen offers your first Fold. It shows exactly what you will keep and what resets before you confirm. Read Folds first, since the whole game builds around it.

    Tip: You do not need to understand everything in your first session. The game explains each system when it unlocks, and nothing you do early on can lock you out of later content.

    While you are away

    Close the game whenever you like. Your workshop keeps producing for up to four hours at full rate, and it shows you what it earned when you return. More in offline progress.

    For faster progress once you have the basics, go to tips for faster progress.
