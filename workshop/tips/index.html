    Tips for faster progress

    Practical, rule-backed advice for the early game, the mid game and every Fold.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. Your first session
    2. Building well
    3. Choosing when to Fold
    4. When you are away
    5. For the active player
    6. For the curious

    None of this is required. It all comes from how the game's rules work.

    Your first session

    • Tap early, tap well. A standard catch is worth at least 1 Moment, and a perfect catch doubles it. When your production is small, your hands are a big share of your income. See catching Moments.
    • Upgrade your first Splitter a few times before building more. A Splitter's output is its level, multiplied by 1.5 for every 25 levels, so levels pay back well.
    • Claim your guide's goals. Each claim pays about two minutes of your current production, with a minimum of 50 Moments. See characters.
    • Buy core sockets early. They cost 25, 100, 300, 750, 1,500, 3,000 and 6,000 Moments in order, and they are permanent across Folds.

    Building well

    • Chain Splitters clockwise in Forward Time. Each Splitter stage feeding a machine gives it +8%, up to +40%. See rings and routes.
    • Add Lenses to your strongest Splitter. Each Lens gives up to +50% and Lenses combined cap at +100%.
    • Level in steps of 25. Each full 25 levels multiplies a Splitter's output by 1.5. Using the x25 button gets you there efficiently.
    • Watch Load, not just production. If Load exceeds capacity, every machine slows. Add an Escapement before another producer. See load and stability.
    • Use Efficient and Overclocked on purpose. Efficient frees capacity, Overclocked uses it for more output. See modifications and blueprints.
    • Save a layout once you have a design you like, so rebuilding after a Fold is quick and the game shows you the cost. Up to twelve layouts can be saved.

    Choosing when to Fold

    • Do not hoard Moments. Spending never reduces what the run has earned, so spend as soon as you can afford something good.
    • Fold when the climb slows. Chronons grow fastest up to the run's peak, then more slowly. Waiting far beyond the peak invites Strain, which makes costs rise. See Folds.
    • Spend Chronons on Temporal yield first. It multiplies all Moment production, and it is the game's own recommendation. See research.
    • Spread research across lines. Each rank costs double the last, so a few ranks of several lines usually beat many ranks of one.

    When you are away

    • Spend before you leave. Your workshop earns for up to four hours at its full rate, so a stronger workshop earns more while you are away.
    • Check Load before closing the game. An overloaded board wastes your offline hours.
    • Take Long memory if you often stay away more than four hours. It extends the limit by 2.5 hours per rank, up to 24 hours. See offline progress.
    • Do not chase Golden seconds offline. They pass while you are away, so claim them when you are playing.

    For the active player

    • Aim for full Rhythm. Ten perfect taps in a row grows your Rhythm streak, fills Resonance faster, and meets the lantern moth.
    • Streak bursts pay well. 10 perfect taps in a row pays 5 seconds of production, 25 pays 15, and every 50 pays 30.
    • Buy Steady Hands, Surge Coil and Golden Instinct for a run that is heavy on tapping. They reset on a Fold, so buy them early.
    • Finish daily tasks that match what you were doing anyway. See daily tasks.

    For the curious

    • Try each temporal law once. Each changes how you build. See temporal laws.
    • Try a Trial after your third Fold. They are optional and make you build without something you normally rely on. See Trials.
    • Explore discoveries. Each one also unlocks blueprints and modifications as the count grows. See discoveries and experiments.

    Tip: If you ever feel stuck, open the Fold screen. It shows your target, your reward, and what you keep.
