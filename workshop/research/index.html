    Research

    Permanent research bought with Chronons, run research bought with Precision, and which upgrades to take first.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. Permanent research (Chronons)
      1. What to take first
    2. Run research (Precision)
    3. Catch upgrades
    4. Where it fits

    There are two kinds of research. They share the Research tab but work differently.

    Permanent research (Chronons)

    Chronons come from Folding, and permanent research survives every Fold. Each rank costs double the one before it, starting from the base cost in the table.

    Research Effect per rank Ranks First rank costs
    Temporal yield +25% Moment production 20 1 Chronon
    Stable foundations +2 capacity per board 10 1
    Long memory +2.5 offline hours, from 4 hours up to 24 8 1
    Sensitive hands +10% catch rewards 10 1
    Perfect optics +10% Lens strength, keeping each Lens cap 5 2
    Fine measurement +10% Precision production 10 1
    Efficient assembly Machine installation and upgrades cost 4% less 5 2
    Resonant memory Loops warm up 10% faster (needs Looping Time) 5 2
    Parallel thought +5% convergence bonus (needs Branching Time) 5 3
    Deep reserves +2% Reservoir release bonus 5 2
    Automatic hands Unlocks a toggle for one standard automatic catch per second 1 2
    Head start Start each Fold with 25 Moments 5 1

    Research cards explain each benefit before you buy.

    What to take first

    The game's own guide suggests Temporal yield first, since it multiplies everything. After that it depends on what is holding you back:

    • Boards straining under load: Stable foundations.
    • Long absences: Long memory. See offline progress.
    • Wanting an easier early run: Efficient assembly and Head start.
    • Interested in the other laws of time: Resonant memory and Parallel thought. See temporal laws.

    Tip: Because each rank doubles in price, spreading Chronons across several research lines is often better than pushing one line to its limit.

    Run research (Precision)

    Run research is bought with Precision from your Archives and resets when you Fold. There are three lines, and each rank costs 10 Precision doubled for every rank already bought.

    Run research Effect per rank Ranks
    Production +10% production 20
    Capacity +2 capacity per board 20
    Catch window +10 milliseconds to the perfect-catch window 8

    Early in a run, an Archive and a few ranks of Production and Capacity pay for themselves quickly. Later on, your Precision keeps accumulating while you are away.

    Catch upgrades

    A third kind of upgrade is bought with Moments: the catch upgrades. These also reset on Fold.

    Where it fits

    Permanent research carries over every Fold, so your next run starts with higher production, a bigger offline allowance and cheaper machines. You reach the target sooner and Fold for more. See Folds.
