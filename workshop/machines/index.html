    Machines

    All twelve machines, what each does, when it unlocks, and how to use it well.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. The twelve machines
    2. The numbers behind output
    3. Producers, supporters and keepers
    4. Phases
    5. Weights and load
    6. Building well

    Your workshop has twelve kinds of machine. Each unlocks once your lifetime Moments reach a threshold. Unlocks are permanent and stay after a Fold. A machine's install cost is the price of placing it at level 1. After that you buy levels, and every level costs 17% more than the last.

    The twelve machines

    Machine Unlocks at (lifetime Moments) What it does
    Splitter Start Produces Moments. Route Splitters clockwise to build a production chain.
    Lens 100 Route it to a producer for +5% plus 1% per square root of its level, up to +50% each. Together, lenses cap at +100%.
    Escapement 200 Adds 3 plus 0.2 per level to this board's stability capacity.
    Archive 300 Produces Precision for run research and for Phase Shifter fuel.
    Bridge 500 Has three outgoing routes and lets rings connect away from the quarter points.
    Conductor 800 Adds a capped bonus to active catches. No route needed.
    Reservoir 1,500 Route a Splitter into it. It stores 20% of that Splitter's output and releases it every 30 seconds with a level bonus.
    Synchroniser 2,500 Route it to a producer with the same phase for +15%, up to +50% combined.
    Phase Shifter 4,000 When enabled, spends Precision every 30 seconds to rotate its target's phase by one.
    Echo Chamber 6,000 Route a Splitter into it. It repeats a fraction of that Splitter's unmodified base output. Echoes cannot echo.
    Anchor 10,000 Preserves some of a loop's resonance when you change the circuit. Combined retention caps at 75%.
    Observatory 15,000 Advances experiments and earns Precision.

    The numbers behind output

    • A Splitter makes its level in Moments per second, multiplied by 1.5 for every full 25 levels. Levels 25 and 50 are worth aiming for.
    • An Archive works the same way for Precision, at 0.02 per level, with a 1.4 multiplier for every 25 levels.
    • Machine levels top out at one million.

    Producers, supporters and keepers

    Machines fall into three roles:

    • Producers make resources: Splitters (Moments), Echo Chambers (extra Moments), Archives (Precision) and Observatories.
    • Supporters strengthen what they are routed to: Lens, Synchroniser, Phase Shifter, Reservoir, Echo Chamber, Conductor.
    • Keepers protect the board: Escapement (capacity), Anchor (loop resonance), Bridge (wiring).

    Phases

    Every machine has a phase, from 0 to 3. A Synchroniser only helps a producer on the same phase. A Phase Shifter spends Precision to rotate a target's phase, and you can switch it on and off. If you are not using Synchronisers, you can ignore phases entirely.

    Weights and load

    Most machines count as 1 toward your board's load. Escapements, Bridges and Anchors count as half. The heavier a machine's level, the more load it adds. See load and stability.

    Building well

    • Early on, more Splitters in a chain beat everything else. See rings and routes.
    • Add a Lens to a strong Splitter before you add a fourth or fifth one.
    • Once the board is straining, install an Escapement instead of another producer.
    • Add an Archive when you want run research. See research.

    Each machine can also carry one modification.

    Tip: Not sure what a machine is for? Tap it on the ring and read its card. It always shows its own description and cost before you buy.
