    Trials of the Held Second

    Ten optional rules you can take on for one run, earn a seal for, and master for up to three stars each.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. How Trials work
    2. Stars
    3. The ten Trials
    4. A voice for each seal
    5. Tips for Trials

    After three Folds, the Fold screen offers an optional extra: a Trial. You pick one rule to follow for the next run. Fold again while the rule still holds and you earn that Trial's seal.

    How Trials work

    • A seal is only recognition. It does not change production, and you never need a Trial.
    • Breaking the rule ends the Trial and only the Trial. Your run carries on normally.
    • The rule is checked after every action and every step of the simulation, so time away is treated the same as time at the bench.
    • Trials never expire or rotate. Take them whenever you like.

    Stars

    A Fold under the rule that pays enough Chronons earns a star. Each Trial has its own goal, and the second star needs 1.5 times the goal and the third needs twice the goal. Each star pays once, in a small reward of Prisms and, for the higher stars, stored time skips. It never pays production or Chronons. You can replay a Trial to earn more stars.

    The ten Trials

    Trial The rule Chronons for the first star
    Single Ring Fold without opening a second ring. Core sockets are fine. 4
    Steady State Fold without the board ever going over capacity. 4
    Lone Splitter Never more than one Splitter on a board. 5
    Minimalist Never more than four machines installed at once. 3
    Purist Fold without giving any machine a modification. 4
    Clear Glass Fold without ever installing a Lens. 5
    Unbraced Fold without ever installing an Escapement. 4
    Short Wiring Never more than three routes at once. 4
    Two Rings Fold without opening a third ring on any board. 5
    Few Kinds Never more than three kinds of machine installed at once. 3

    The second and third stars need those goals multiplied by 1.5 and 2, rounded up. For example, Single Ring wants 4, 6 and 8 Chronons.

    A voice for each seal

    Each Trial ends with a comment from Iona or Mara. For Single Ring, Iona says: "One ring, and you made it sing. I will stop calling that bench too small."

    Tips for Trials

    • Steady State is a good first Trial. Add an Escapement early and keep an eye on Load. See load and stability.
    • Minimalist and Few Kinds reward upgrading a handful of machines deeply. Levels in multiples of 25 give a 1.5x multiplier to a Splitter's output. See machines.
    • Clear Glass and Unbraced mean doing without a favourite tool, so plan for that from the start of the run.
    • Because a Trial asks for a large Chronon payout, the stars suit a run where you let the board grow towards its peak. See Folds.
    • Take on a Trial in a law you know well. See temporal laws.

    Note: If you accidentally break a Trial's rule, carry on with the run. You can try it again at your next Fold.
