    Load and stability

    Why a busy board slows down, how to read the Load meter, and the ways to give your workshop room to breathe.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. What the numbers mean
    2. How load is counted
    3. Raising capacity
    4. How big is the penalty?
    5. Strain: when a long run gets heavy
    6. Reading the situation

    Above the clock, you will see a Load readout such as Load 8.2 / 8.0. Once your workshop gets busy, this is the number to watch.

    What the numbers mean

    • Load is how hard your machines are working the board. Every machine adds to it, and higher levels add more.
    • Capacity is how much the board can bear. It starts at 8.
    • Efficiency is how well your machines run. At or under capacity it is 100%.

    If Load exceeds capacity, every machine gives a little less. Nothing breaks and nothing is lost, but all the machines run slower together. The game's own wording is: more machines are not always better.

    How load is counted

    Each machine adds a weight, multiplied by a factor that grows as it levels up (2% per level). Escapements, Bridges and Anchors count as half-weight machines. Everything else counts as one.

    The Load meter responds to modifications too:

    • Efficient reduces a machine's load by 30%.
    • Overclocked makes a machine produce 30% more, but adds 50% more load.

    See modifications and blueprints.

    Raising capacity

    Source Effect
    Escapement machines +3 plus 0.2 per level, each
    Stable foundations research +2 per rank, permanent
    Capacity run research +2 per rank, this run only

    An Escapement is the usual answer to a straining board. Its capacity rises as you level it.

    How big is the penalty?

    When load goes over capacity, efficiency falls as the overshoot grows. If you are at 1.5 times capacity, efficiency is 80%. The slope is gradual, but it adds up when you are far over.

    Strain: when a long run gets heavy

    A separate effect called Strain affects very long runs. Once a run has earned well past its peak point, which is twice the point where Chronon gains start to slow, machine costs begin to rise. Strain resets with every Fold. It is the game's way of telling you the climb has slowed and a Fold is due. See folds.

    Reading the situation

    • The Load chip turns amber and shows a warning mark when you are over.
    • Tap Load above the ring and the game offers help.
    • Some story goals and one of the Trials care about load too.

    Tip: If you are only slightly over capacity, a few extra levels on an Escapement are cheaper than losing efficiency across the board.

    Warning: Adding another Splitter is not always an improvement. If it pushes you far over capacity, it can lower your total output.
