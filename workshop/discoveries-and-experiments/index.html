    Discoveries and experiments

    All 24 discoveries and how to earn them, plus the four Experiment Board challenges.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. The full list
    2. Easy ones for early on
    3. Harder ones
    4. The Experiment Board

    Discoveries are milestones the game records as you play. There are 24, they are found under More, and they never expire or reset. Each one can be collected for a small Prism reward.

    The number you have found unlocks blueprints and modifications, and a few open story chapters.

    The full list

    Group Discoveries
    Moments earned (lifetime) First hundred; A thousand moments; Ten thousand moments; A hundred thousand; Million moments; Billion moments
    Machines installed Two machines; Four machines; Eight machines; Sixteen machines
    Kinds of machine Three disciplines; Six disciplines; Nine disciplines; All twelve disciplines
    Precision First Precision; Precision reserve: 100
    Catches Ten catches; One hundred catches
    Laws A living circuit (a closed loop in Looping Time); Parallel clocks (a Splitter on the second branch in Branching Time)
    Folds First Fold; Five Folds
    Expansion Orbital expansion; Horizon expansion

    "Machines installed" and "kinds of machine" both count what is in your workshop at one time. Once a discovery is recorded, it is yours for good, even after a Fold clears the board.

    Easy ones for early on

    • Tap ten times to get Ten catches.
    • Install a second Splitter for Two machines.
    • Build an Archive for First Precision.
    • Open the Orbital ring for Orbital expansion. It also introduces a new character.

    Harder ones

    • All twelve disciplines needs one of every machine kind installed together, so plan sockets for it.
    • Horizon expansion needs the third ring on a board.
    • A living circuit and Parallel clocks need you to choose those laws at a Fold.

    The Experiment Board

    Alongside your discoveries under More, an Experiment Board offers four challenges. Each asks you to hold a condition for 120 uninterrupted seconds, and then you claim 50 Precision. You can repeat any of them.

    Experiment The challenge
    Quiet engineering Run at least four machines without any Escapement and without overload.
    Clockwise cascade In Forward Time, keep a chain with three upstream Splitter stages.
    Closed eternity In Looping Time, keep at least six machines in closed circuits.
    Perfect counterparts In Branching Time, keep both branches' production within 10% of each other.

    The Looping and Branching experiments stay hidden until you can choose those laws.

    Experiments are optional. Observatory machines also advance experiments and earn Precision. See machines.

    Tip: The Experiment Board is a good warm-up for the Trials, which ask for similar restraint across a whole run.
