    Folds

    The prestige system explained. When to Fold, what resets, what you keep, how Chronons are earned and how each Fold changes the next run.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. Your first Fold
    2. What you keep and what you reset
    3. How Chronons are earned
      1. When to Fold
    4. Each Fold raises the bar
    5. Choosing your next run
    6. Story and Folds

    A Fold is the game's prestige system. You take your finished workshop apart on purpose, receive Chronons, and rebuild under a new law of time. In the story's own words, the workshop "comes apart without breaking. Every screw returns to the bench; every lesson remains in your hands."

    Your first Fold

    Your first Fold becomes possible at 2.5 million Moments earned in a single run, and it pays your first Chronon. The Fold tab shows your target, the reward you would receive right now, and what resets, before you confirm anything.

    What you keep and what you reset

    You keep You reset
    Chronons Moments and Precision
    Permanent research Machines and their levels
    Discoveries and saved layouts Run research and rings
    Core sockets and Prisms Catch upgrades
    Story progress, characters met Rhythm, Surge and Golden timers

    After a Fold you start again with a single Splitter on each board, in the law and blueprint you chose. If you took Head start research, you also begin with a small stock of Moments.

    How Chronons are earned

    The more Moments you earned in the run, the more Chronons you receive, but the curve changes shape:

    • Up to a peak point, Chronons grow fast, rising with the square of your Moments.
    • Past the peak, gains slow down but never stop.
    • On a first run the peak is about 7.9 million Moments. It rises with every Chronon you hold, so it keeps moving up as you progress.

    The game reminds you of this on the screen: Chronons grow fastest until a certain number of run-earned Moments, then more slowly. Fold when the climb slows.

    When to Fold

    Fold around the peak or shortly after. Waiting much longer than that pays only a little more, and if you run well past it, Strain makes machine costs climb. See load and stability. Folding early isn't punished, but you can't farm it either, since every Chronon raises the peak equally however you earned it.

    Tip: Fold when your Moments per second stops climbing noticeably. Your next run will start faster because of what you just earned.

    Each Fold raises the bar

    Because your peak rises with your lifetime Chronons, the target for each new Fold grows too. In return, your permanent research makes every run faster, so reaching the new target takes less effort than the last one did.

    Choosing your next run

    On the Fold screen you choose:

    1. A temporal law: Forward, Looping or Branching. See temporal laws. Looping unlocks at 15 lifetime Chronons and Branching at 60. The Fold you are about to take counts toward those numbers.
    2. A blueprint. See modifications and blueprints.
    3. Optionally, after your third Fold, a Trial. See trials.

    Story and Folds

    Folding is tied to the story. The first Fold introduces the Counter, Meridia's timekeeper. Further transmissions arrive at later Folds, including at your seventh and eighteenth. See characters.

    Note: Spending Moments does not reduce your run total, so you never need to hoard Moments to reach the target. Spend freely.
