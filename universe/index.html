    The BlairsLab Universe

    One lamplit world, many small games.

    • The games
    • The world
    • Recurring things
    • Where games touch

    Every BlairsLab game is set in the same country. It has towns on branch lines, hop fields, a wharf, a flooded valley, a moor and a city that has been stopped at one second to midnight. Some games send you travelling across it, and others stay inside a single tower, belfry or workshop. In all of them time is a material, and craftspeople can wind it, hold it and rewind it.

    You don't need to know any of this to play. It's here if you're curious.

    The rules we keep

    We set ourselves a few rules so that the shared world never becomes homework.

    • Every game is complete on its own. You never need another game's story to follow the one you're playing.
    • Links between games are small. You might see a city in the distance, find a letter or notice a creature passing through. One game's story never causes or explains another's.
    • Owning more than one game gets you nothing extra. There are no cross-save bonuses, codes, shared currencies or locked doors.
    • People and memories are never currency or loot in any game.
    • You are never punished for missing a day.
    • The lantern moth is in every game, and it looks the same in all of them.
    • The stories are about tradespeople and neighbours. There is no chosen hero and no gore.
