    Tips for Deeper Waves

    Practical habits that add waves, from reading the Rust to spending brass at camp.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. Reading the field
    2. Spending well
    3. Picking skills
    4. Between runs

    Note The Belfry Gun is in closed testing. Tips are based on how the game works today and may need adjusting as it changes.

    The Rust grows about 11% tougher every wave and your wall takes about 8.5% more damage. Nothing you carry keeps up forever, so these tips push the end later.

    Reading the field

    • Match your gun to the foe. Scatterguns and big guns sweep Swarmlings. Rifles punch through Plated armour. Pistols mop up the small and quick.
    • Kill what shoots first. Spitters and Gargoyles damage the wall from range, so they are often the priority even when something bigger is closer.
    • Learn the boss habits (split, charge, summon, armour). Each has a clear answer, covered in The Rust.
    • Lead your shots with the big gun. Its burst lands where you aim, so aim ahead of moving targets.

    Spending well

    • Spend brass at every camp. Unspent brass is lost when the wall falls, so put it into the wall and your damage.
    • Balance the wall and the gun. Wall stones and plating buy time; damage upgrades end waves sooner. See Skills and the workshop.
    • Buy hens early. They lay eggs every visit, so a hen bought sooner pays more times. See Belcourt, between waves.
    • Use the town hours wisely. You get three per visit. Supper (+50% XP for five waves) helps when you want skills, and prayer (the wall takes 20% less for five waves) helps before a boss.
    • Compare gear properly. The Pack screen shows what each piece does to your damage and the wall. Trust that over the rarity colour.

    Picking skills

    • Gun-specific skills only work for the gun you carry. If you plan to swap guns, think before you pick.
    • When the list runs out, veteran perks fill in. They are small but permanent for the run, so even late levels help.
    • Take a wall skill when the wall is hurting. Extra Stones and Thick Skin are good picks.

    Between runs

    • Spend medals every time. Even a short run pays a few, and the Belfry's Memory compounds. See The Belfry's Memory.
    • Chase the milestones. Heavy Rounds at wave 8, burning loot at wave 11 and the second gunner at wave 15 are all worth working towards. See Unlocks and characters.
    • Try other characters. Some bring a second moth, a free helper or a stronger wall.

    Tip The moth only mends once per run (some characters and one named charm bring it back again).

    Warning Closing the app mid-wave only loses what happened since your last camp, so stop after a camp if you can.
