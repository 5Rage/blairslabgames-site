    Guns and Loot

    Seven rarities, six gear slots, the traits each can roll and the named one-of-a-kind pieces you might find.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. Rarities
    2. Six slots
    3. How big are the rolls?
    4. Gun names
    5. One-of-a-kind pieces
    6. Tips

    Note The Belfry Gun is live on Google Play and still being updated, so the numbers and details here are today's and may change.

    The Rust drops loot as it falls. Tougher kinds drop it more often (a Plated drops more than a Crawler), bosses always do, and elites drop it far more often. Each piece has a rarity, a slot, and a handful of rolled traits that make it unique. A piece's level follows the wave it dropped on, and higher levels are stronger: each level adds 5% on top of the last.

    Rarities

    Rarity Rolled traits Strength vs Common Break-down value (x Common) Opens at
    Common 0 1.00 1 From the start
    Fine 1 1.15 2.5 From the start
    Rare 2 1.32 6 From the start
    Heirloom 3 1.52 15 From the start
    Masterwork 4 1.75 40 Reach wave 30
    Relic 5 2.00 100 Reach wave 60
    Legendary 6 2.30 250 Reach wave 100

    Rarer pieces drop far less often. The three highest rarities open up as unlocks.

    Six slots

    Slot What it can roll
    Gun Damage, fire rate, reload, magazine, crit, pierce, chain, burning, slowing, brass found, knock-back
    Coat Wall health, repair, damage, brass found, wall plating
    Gloves Fire rate, reload, magazine, crit
    Charm Crit, loot luck, XP, brass found, damage, wall plating
    Boots Reload, repair, wall health, wall plating, knock-back, XP
    Ring Damage, crit, fire rate, loot luck, brass found, XP

    Scatterguns can also roll extra pellets and big guns can roll bigger bursts.

    How big are the rolls?

    Each trait rolls inside a range at Common, and grows with rarity. A few examples of the Common range:

    Trait Common range
    Damage +6% to +14%
    Fire rate +5% to +12%
    Reload speed +8% to +18%
    Magazine +15% to +35%
    Crit chance +2% to +5%
    Burning 15% to 30%
    Slowing 15% to 25%
    Wall health +6% to +14%
    Repair (after each wave) 2% to 5%
    Loot luck +8% to +20%
    Wall plating 2% to 5% less damage

    Whole-number traits (pierce, chain and extra pellets) do not scale with rarity. Burning and slowing only appear on loot once you have unlocked them.

    Gun names

    Each gun class has seven names, one per rarity.

    Class Common to Legendary
    Pistol Pocket Pistol, Clerk's Pistol, Duelling Pistol, Belcourt Repeater, The Minute Hand, Clocktower Duellist, Midnight Pistol
    Rifle Longarm, Hunter's Rifle, Surveyor's Rifle, Steeple Rifle, The Long Hour, Meridian Longarm, Far Meridian Rifle
    Scattergun Blunderbuss, Coach Gun, Fowling Piece, Belfry Scatter, The Peal, Stormcaller Scatter, Thunderpeal
    Big gun Hand Mortar, Chimney Mortar, Lantern Mortar, Bellmouth, The Great Toll, Foundry Mortar, Bellfounder's Mortar

    One-of-a-kind pieces

    From Masterwork upwards, a piece may roll as a named unique with its own special effect. Here are a few of them.

    Piece Slot Effect
    Hester's First Pistol Shots chain to two more Rust; crits mend the wall a little
    The Long Hour Rifle Shots pierce everything in their path
    The Peal Scattergun Every pellet bursts where it hits
    The Great Toll Big gun Bursts stop the Rust dead for a second
    The Iron Cloak Coat The wall takes 15% less damage
    The First Chime Charm The moth comes once more each run
    Hands of the Hour Gloves +15% fire rate and +10% damage
    Seven-League Boots Boots Reload 25% faster, and shots knock the Rust back
    The Founder's Signet Ring +15% damage and +10% crit chance

    There are more.

    Tips

    Tip Compare before you wear. The Pack screen shows how each piece changes your damage and the wall, so trust the numbers over the colour.

    Tip A lower-rarity piece with the right trait can beat a higher one with the wrong one. A rifle that pierces helps most against armour.

    Broken-down loot turns into brass. See Skills and the workshop for what brass buys, and Belcourt, between waves for where you do it.
