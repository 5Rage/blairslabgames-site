    From the same world

    How The Belfry Gun connects to the rest of the Evenwold.

    Updated 16 November 2026

    On this page
    1. Your corner of the world
    2. The lantern moth
    3. Familiar faces
    4. The other games

    Note The Belfry Gun is in testing, so details here may change.

    The Belfry Gun is set in the Evenwold, the small country shared by every Evenwold game. You do not need any other game to play it.

    Your corner of the world

    Belcourt is a hill town under a great belfry, just outside the edge of Meridia's held second. The Rust gathers where time has been left alone, and here it keeps coming at the wall. See Belcourt, between waves and The Rust.

    The lantern moth

    The moth appears in every Evenwold game. Here it settles on the wall once a run and mends it when it is nearly gone. It is never a weapon or for sale, and nothing bad happens to it while you are away.

    Familiar faces

    Some people from the Workshop's city have come out to help hold the wall. See Unlocks and characters.

    The other games

    • The Last Second Workshop: Meridia itself, held at 23:59:59.
    • Hivewright: climb a great clock tower with a hive of wind-ups.
    • The Last Bell: a different bell in a different valley.
    • The Lamplighter: the road to the city wall (closed testing).
    • The Roads Between: small towns on the back roads (closed testing).
