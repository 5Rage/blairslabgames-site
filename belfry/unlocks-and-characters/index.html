    Unlocks and Characters

    The milestones that add variety to every later run, and the sixteen characters who can stand on the wall.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. Milestones
    2. Sixteen characters
      1. Characters who join later

    Note The game is in closed testing. Milestones and perks may change before release.

    In The Belfry Gun your gun, loot, brass and workshop all start from nothing every run. Only three things last:

    1. Records.
    2. Unlocks, which are milestones that add skills, loot traits and more to later runs.
    3. The Belfry's Memory, deep permanent upgrades bought with medals. See its own page.

    Unlocks add more options to later runs.

    Milestones

    Reach the wave shown in a run and the unlock is yours for good.

    Reach wave You unlock What it does
    8 Heavy Rounds A new skill: shots knock the Rust back
    11 Burning loot Guns can roll burning
    13 Hot Lead A new skill: hits set the Rust burning
    15 The second gunner The workshop can hire a helper from wave 10
    18 Slowing loot Guns can roll slowing
    20 A gun's second skill Hollow Points (pistol), Plate Breaker (rifle), Slug Shells (scattergun) or Black Powder (big gun), when you reach it with that gun
    25 Chaining loot Guns can roll chain
    30 Masterwork The rarest loot can drop
    60 Relic Even rarer loot can drop
    100 Legendary The rarest loot of all can drop

    Sixteen characters

    Each character has a favourite gun, a strength and a trade-off. Eight are open from the start, and eight more join later.

    Character Gun Perk
    Hester Bell, the old gunner Pistol +10% wall, repairs 2% after every wave
    Senn, courier Pistol +20% fire rate, +25% brass, -10% wall
    Edda Rook, lighthouse keeper Rifle Shots pierce one more, -8% fire rate
    Astronomer Vey Rifle +12% crit and starts with Keen Eye, -20% magazine
    Nell Mason, stonemason Scattergun +30% wall, the wall takes 15% less, -5% fire rate
    Brannoch, bandleader Scattergun +1 pellet, +10% damage, shots knock the Rust well back
    Brother Anselm, verger Big gun The moth comes twice a run, +5% wall
    Odile Marr, retired lamplighter Big gun Shells set the Rust burning, reloads 10% slower

    These eight are open from the start.

    Characters who join later

    Each of the other eight joins when its partner character reaches wave 200, so you need to play several characters to unlock them all.

    Joins When this character reaches wave 200 Perk
    Mara Vale, signal operator Senn +8% crit, crits hit harder, -8% damage
    Pip Tallow, steeplejack Hester Starts with Fan the Hammer, reloads 15% faster, -10% damage
    The Keeper Edda +30% loot luck, loot two levels better
    Clerk Abernethy Vey +35% XP (more skills), -10% damage
    Ilse, bandleader Brannoch Repairs 5% after every wave, +8% damage, +15% brass
    Fitch Nell Biscuit the terrier helps from wave 1 as a second gunner
    Iona, clockmaker Odile The workshop costs 25% less, +10% burst size
    Old Tobin, gunsmith Anselm +30% burst size, +10% damage, breaking down loot pays double

    Some of these faces come from The Last Second Workshop and are only passing through Belcourt; others are Belcourt locals. Playing one game gives you nothing in the other.

    A gun's own skills only work while you carry that kind of gun, so a character's gun matters.
