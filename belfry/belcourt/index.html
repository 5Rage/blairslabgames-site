    Belcourt, Between Waves

    Every fifth wave the gunner makes camp in Belcourt to wear loot, shop, eat, pray, keep hens and spend brass.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. In town
    2. The pack
    3. The workshop
    4. Settling in

    Note The Belfry Gun is live on Google Play and still being updated, so the numbers and details here are today's and may change.

    After waves 5, 10, 15 and every fifth wave beyond, the fighting stops. Nothing moves until you leave. You are in Belcourt, and you have three hours to spend before heading back up to the wall.

    Everything in town resets when a run ends. Brass is for this run only.

    In town

    Place What it does
    The Bell Inn Supper costs brass and gives +50% XP for the next five waves.
    The chapel Praying makes the wall take 20% less damage for the next five waves.
    The market Restock it for three pieces of gear, a level better than what drops, bought with brass.
    Searching Go looking in the dark streets. You might find brass, loot, a stray hen, a lesson (a free skill pick), or nothing.
    The hen house Buy hens with brass, each a little dearer than the last. Hens lay eggs worth brass at every visit.

    Several of these cost hours (supper and prayer take an hour each), so you cannot do everything every visit.

    The pack

    The Pack tab shows the gear you have found. For each piece, the game shows how wearing it would change your damage and the wall. You can:

    • Wear it.
    • Break it down into brass. Better rarities pay far more (see Guns and loot).

    Your pack holds 24 pieces. If it fills, the game breaks down the weakest piece automatically so you never miss a drop.

    The workshop

    The Workshop tab is where brass becomes wall stones, plating, a mortar crew, a second gunner and more. Full list in Skills and the workshop.

    Settling in

    • The run is saved at every camp.
    • There is a "bring the next wave now" button between waves for the impatient.
    • The Belfry's Memory can start later runs partway up the hill. See The Belfry's Memory.

    Tip Hens pay back slowly, so they are best bought early. A hen bought on your first visit lays eggs on every later visit.
