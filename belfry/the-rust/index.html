    The Rust

    Every kind of clockwork foe, the elite twists, the twelve places on the hill and the bosses that guard them.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. The kinds
    2. Elites
    3. Twelve places, round and round
    4. Four boss habits

    Note The Belfry Gun is in closed testing. Foe numbers are today's and may change.

    The kinds

    The health below is for wave 1. It grows by about 11% every wave.

    Kind Health Habit
    Crawler 20 Plain walker. The bulk of every wave.
    Skitter 10 Small and quick, zig-zagging up the hill.
    Moth-eater 14 Flies, and weaves from side to side.
    Plated 60 Slow and armoured: every hit loses 6 damage. Big single hits get through.
    Spitter 24 Stops halfway and spits rust at the wall from range.
    Swarmling 6 Arrives in clumps of six. Scatterguns and big guns sweep them up.
    Roller 34 A plated wheel that rolls straight at the wall, fast. (Clockmakers' Quarter onward.)
    Gargoyle 30 Flies down from the rooftops, weaves, and spits from high up the hill. (High Wall only.)

    Tip Armour takes a flat amount off every hit, so ten tiny hits are worse than one big one. Rifles and big guns do well against Plated Rust.

    Elites

    From wave 12, some Rust turn up elite. The chance climbs with depth, and by deep waves up to about a third of a wave can be elite. Elites have 1.5 times the health, but pay triple brass, double XP and drop loot far more often. There are four kinds, and they open up as you go deeper.

    Elite Twist
    Shielded A shield soaks up its first hits.
    Swift It moves much faster.
    Splitting It splits in two when it falls.
    Mending It mends the Rust around it.

    Twelve places, round and round

    Every ten waves the fight moves to a new place in town, each with its own mix of Rust and its own boss. There are twelve places in three districts. After the twelfth, the hill starts over, with much tougher Rust.

    Place District Boss The boss's habit
    Orchard Steps The Lower Hill The Bell-Breaker Split it, and both halves keep coming
    Mill Lane The Lower Hill The Millstone Rolls in fits and starts; watch for the charge
    Tanners' Row The Lower Hill The Rag Choir Sings, and Skitters come running
    The Old Gate The Lower Hill The Gatewarden Heavily plated; hit it hard, not often
    Spring Street Clockmakers' Quarter The Mainspring Uncoils in sudden lunges
    Escapement Yard Clockmakers' Quarter The Two-Faced Clock Split it, and both halves keep coming
    Gearwright's Lane Clockmakers' Quarter The Tin Cantor Sings, and Skitters come running
    The Pendulum Bridge Clockmakers' Quarter The Iron Verger Heavily plated; hit it hard, not often
    Rookery Roofs The High Wall The Rook King Sings, and Skitters come running
    Lamp Row The High Wall The Snuffer Rolls in fits and starts; watch for the charge
    The Bell Stair The High Wall The Cracked Bell Split it, and both halves keep coming
    Belfry Gate The High Wall The Rustwright Plated, and it calls the Rust to it

    Bosses have 420 health at wave 1 (scaled up by depth), carry a little armour and always drop loot. Every boss beaten also pays a medal toward the Belfry's Memory.

    Four boss habits

    Every boss uses one of four tricks:

    • Split. Kill it and it becomes two. Keep your fire up.
    • Charge. It stalls, then lunges. Save your best shots for the lunge.
    • Summon. It calls Skitters. Clear the escort or the wall pays for it.
    • Armour. A thick plate, so big single hits and armour-ignoring skills work best.

    See also Tips for deeper waves and Guns and loot.
