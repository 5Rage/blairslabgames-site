    Wicklow Halt and the Old Cutting

    The station village where you start, the road east of it, and a dark disused railway tunnel.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. At a glance
    2. Rust here
    3. Chests and secrets
    4. Side stories and errands
    5. Tips

    Wicklow Halt runs to the station clock, and in forty years the 6:12 has never been late. You start in the post office on a morning that has gone very quiet. Outside, something is wrong at the station, and a small moth of soft light wants to show you the way.

    The Wicklow Road runs east from the village, and a side path climbs to the Old Cutting, a disused railway tunnel. Rust has got in there and it is dark enough that you will want the moth's Glow.

    At a glance
    Where Wicklow Post Office, Wicklow Halt, Penhallow's Stores, The Halt Inn, The Wicklow Road, The Old Cutting
    Who you can meet Aunt Agnes, Old Jory, Baker Tull, Mrs Amberley, Dot, Pim, Constable Fenn, Mrs Penhallow, Hettie, a traveller at the inn, Farmer Mossop. Corwin is waiting near the platform and will walk with you from there.
    Shops and inns Penhallow's Stores (Mrs Penhallow) sells Tonics (12 Marks), Oil Cans (10), Wind Draughts (20) and Spark Jars (30). The Halt Inn (Hettie) offers a bed for 10 Marks. More on the Shops page.
    Rest and save The bed in Wicklow Post Office. A camp on The Wicklow Road, which appears once Corwin is with you. A save clock in the middle of The Old Cutting.
    Moth Light Glow, from the very start. It lights dark places and shows hidden ways, and in battle it warns you a turn early when a foe is about to use a BIG move.

    Rust here

    The Rust are visible on the map and there are no random battles, so you choose every fight. Almost everything in this chapter is weak to Spark, which is Corwin's element, so lean on him. Full stat tables are on Wicklow to Tollbridge Rust.

    Rust Where Weak to
    Rust Crawler Halt lane, Wicklow Road, Old Cutting Spark
    Skitter Wicklow Road, Old Cutting Nothing, but small, quick and hard to hit
    Spitter Wicklow Road, Old Cutting Spark (its spit can leave a friend Rusting)
    Clinker Old Cutting Spark (tougher plates, resists Gear)

    An optional elite, Sergeant Clinker, patrols the east end of The Wicklow Road and the west end of The Old Cutting with two Skitters. It is weak to Spark, resists Gear, and does not come back once beaten, so it is a good reward for a bit of extra training. A boss waits at the far end of the tunnel.

    Chests and secrets

    All of this chapter's chests are in The Old Cutting and none are hidden.

    • Spark Jar
    • 2 x Tonic
    • Lucky Stamp
    • Brass Rod
    • 40 Marks

    There is a shimmering patch of wall in the tunnel. The moth's Glow shows a way through it.

    Side stories and errands

    Errand Where What to do Reward
    Mossop's Field The Wicklow Road, Farmer Mossop Clear the Rust from his fenced field west of the farm road: two Skitters and a Rust Crawler, in one fight. Go back and tell him. Brass Button and 30 Marks
    Wren's first round Wicklow Post Office, Aunt Agnes Late in the chapter Agnes hands you three letters to carry on to the Hopfields. You deliver them in the next chapter. 50 Marks when all three are delivered

    Tips

    • Bring Spark. Corwin's Arc and Spark Jars do most of the work here.
    • Skitters are fast and slippery. Deal with the Crawlers and Spitters first, or finish Skitters with a steady hit rather than a gamble.
    • Rest at the save clock in the tunnel before you push on to the end. The ordinary Rust come back after you rest, so you can train a little if the road feels hard.
    • Keep a few Tonics for the boss, and an Oil Can for Rusting.

    The full story of this chapter is on Chapter 1 plot and boss, with spoilers.
