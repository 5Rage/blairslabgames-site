    Chapter 10 plot and boss

    Wren delivers a thirty-year-old letter, the moth learns Dawn, the Stilling Engine is tired out, and the Hour Clock is wound at last.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    Spoilers ahead.This page discusses story events and reveals. Come back after you have played if you want to find things out yourself.
    On this page
    1. What happens
    2. Party and moth
    3. Boss: The Stilling Engine
    4. What the chapter leaves you with

    Warning This page gives away the plot and the boss of this chapter. It also gives away the ending of the first half of the game.

    For the places, chests and Rust, see The Hour Road.

    What happens

    The balloon sets you down above a road that runs east as far as anyone can see. Ottoline explains that every clock in the Evenwold was once set along it. Hob will wait and a lamp will call him whenever you want a lift. Carvings on the three waystations say that at the first stone the hour was set, that every town keeps its own hour but keeps it for the others, and that at the end of the road there is a clock to be wound kindly, for it is old and has been very patient.

    In a neat little house sits Master Aldous Venn, the old clockmaker, looking at a clock that does not tick. He greets Corwin with "You came" and takes in the rest of you: a courier, a bargewoman, a scholar and a moth. Wren hands over the dead letter that has been in her satchel since Tollbridge, addressed "A., Hollowmere". It is from Marta, in her own hand. She asks him not to keep it but to keep her, by letting the clocks tick; she would rather be missed than stopped, and asks him to wind something for her on Sunday. Aldous never knew she wrote. He says it was never lost, only kept.

    He explains why every clock stopped at twelve minutes past six: it was the minute the sluice gates opened above Hollowmere, and he could never choose another. He admits the Stilling Engine cannot be turned off, because he built it so that nothing could. It only holds what the clocks hold. He stilled the first clocks by hand to give it something to grip, so every clock you mended was a grip lost, which is why it is tired. He left a word in each clock for whoever came. He cannot turn the Engine off, but he can wind the Hour Clock with Marta's words in his pocket, and asks to walk with you.

    The moth's light changes from a lantern's glow to something like the first light of morning. It has learned Dawn, and the road east opens.

    At the end of the road stands the Hour Clock, hands halted at the top of the hour. In front of it hums the Stilling Engine, one tired note. Corwin says it is not angry, it just cannot stop. Aldous warns that it will try to stop you, "nothing personal". Wren answers that you are not here to break it, only to let the world start again. When it is beaten, the Engine sets itself down in a long exhale of steam, like the Porter did.

    Aldous walks to the Clock with his brass key and Marta's letter tucked in his sleeve. "I stopped a village once. I told myself it was love. It was only fear with a good reason." Corwin steadies the key, Wren turns it, Aldous covers their hands, and the moth circles the great face. The hands move, and the hour rings along the whole road. Everywhere answers: the station clock at Wicklow Halt and Aunt Agnes's kettle, the mill sails in the Hopfields, the happy arguing on Tollbridge, lamps in the Underway, the tide on time at Saltmarsh, autumn in the Glasshouse, the chapel clock under Hollowmere's water "thirty years late", sheep on the High Moor and hammers in the Cinder Works. Aldous says he will go down to Hollowmere on Sunday and wind that clock himself.

    The Engine now ticks softly. The moth lands on Wren's shoulder and stays. Aldous remembers Marta saying that a lamp-moth chooses whoever is carrying other people's words. The party plans to head home by the Belfry, then on to Saltmarsh, with Ottoline off to "a book to not finish". Far off a bell rings the hour, and the card reads "The roads go on...".

    Party and moth

    • Nobody joins or leaves. Aldous walks with you through the closing scenes but is not a party member.
    • The moth learns Dawn, its sixth Light. In battle it heals and steadies everyone once per battle and never attacks. Save it for after a big hit. See The moth's Lights.

    Boss: The Stilling Engine

    Aldous Venn's great machine, a clock that tries to stop every other clock. It is the toughest fight so far.
    HP 30,000
    Attack 182
    Defence 38
    Mind 174
    Speed 21
    XP / Marks 7,000 / 3,000
    Weak to Spark and Tide
    Resists Gear
    Drops Wind Draught (always)

    These are base figures. The game tunes bosses higher, so expect a stiff fight. Train a few extra fights first; the Rust returns after a rest.

    Moves

    Move What it does
    Piston An ordinary hit on one hero. Its most common move
    Still the Hands A weak magic hit on everyone; about a third chance to Seize for 1 turn
    Slow Hour A magic hit on everyone; about 60 percent chance to Slow for 2 turns
    Cold Boiler A magic hit on one hero; about 70 percent chance of Rusting for 3 turns
    Wind Down It hurries itself so it acts sooner. No damage
    Everything at Rest BIG. A heavy blow on the whole party
    The Last Stroke BIG. The hardest single-target hit; it comes twice per cycle

    The moth's Glow shows both BIG moves a turn early. The fixed 14-move cycle runs Piston, Slow Hour, Cold Boiler, Piston, Wind Down, Everything at Rest, Still the Hands, Piston, The Last Stroke, and then Slow Hour, Piston, Cold Boiler, Everything at Rest and a second Last Stroke before repeating.

    Advice. Corwin's Arc (Spark) and Bett's Undertow (Tide, which can also Slow it) hit both weaknesses. The Chains Steam Burst (Corwin and Bett, Tide) and Charged Delivery (Wren and Corwin, Spark) hit every foe. Avoid Gear: it resists it. Ottoline's Expose makes it take more damage from everyone, and Read shows every move. Use Bulwark before Everything at Rest, and Drop Anchor to pull The Last Stroke onto Bett, who has the most HP and Defence. Poultice, Thaw, Oil Cans and Pepper Soup cure Rusting and Seizing. Bring plenty of Lake Tonic and rest at the Hour Clock's save clock before you speak to the Engine. See Battle basics.

    What the chapter leaves you with

    • Marta's letter delivered, the Engine tired out and the Hour Clock ticking again.
    • The moth's Dawn, and a quieter, calmer Master Venn.
    • A road that now leads on east, to Larkhill.
