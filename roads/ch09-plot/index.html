    Chapter 9 plot and boss

    The Hush returns to the Cinder Works, the Engine's empty cradle is found, the town's clock is wound, and Pip leaves as the balloon lifts.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    Spoilers ahead.This page discusses story events and reveals. Come back after you have played if you want to find things out yourself.
    On this page
    1. What happens
    2. Party and moth
    3. Boss: The Hush, Returned
    4. What the chapter leaves you with

    Warning This page gives away the plot and the boss of this chapter.

    For the places, shops, chests and Rust, see The Cinder Works.

    What happens

    The rail-cart stops at a halt where the rails run into a wall of cold chimneys. A whole street of people stands frozen mid-step. Corwin remembers the place from a visit long ago with his master, when the noise was so loud you could not hear yourself think. Ottoline says the Hush has "come home", just as Corwin warned.

    In the hangar, Hob Fenner tells you about the Long Hope, the last balloon in the Evenwold. Her furnace has been cold since the Hush came through. Relight it, and he can fly you anywhere with a high place to land on. On the pad, the moth hovers over the furnace until the pilot light catches and the balloon shivers on her ropes. Hob is delighted. (He will matter again: see Hob.)

    Inside the Foundry the air is warm and a thin grey fog lies between the cold furnaces. Corwin warns that it listens. At the far end the fog stands up, glowing like a coal under ash. It has drunk the foundry's warmth. Corwin says it is stronger at home, and Wren answers that so are they.

    When the Hush settles into the floor, it leaves behind a great empty cradle of iron: the place where the Stilling Engine was built, and is no longer. A brass plate reads that it was built by A. Venn, with thanks to everyone who helped, and asks you not to wind it. Underneath, in a tired hand, the same writer has added that he has gone to the Hour Clock and that the road is long and quiet. Corwin guesses the Engine has been sent down the Hour Road.

    Back in town, Pip holds the ladder, Corwin climbs, Wren passes up his tools and the moth circles the chimney clock. Smoke rises, a hundred hammers begin, and Mrs Slade's bread is still warm. Bett calls it the loudest thing she has ever been happy to hear.

    Party and moth

    • Pip leaves the party as the balloon lifts. His gran wants her moor post delivered and he has been high enough for one year. Wren promises to visit the Belfry, "a real one, this time". See Senn and Pip.
    • Nobody joins. The moth gains no new Light; its existing Light, Kindle, lights the furnace.
    • The balloon becomes your fast travel between high places. From the air Hob points out where the world is still keeping time: a bell in the valley, a lamp on the east road, a tower on the moor, a wall on a hill and, far off, a light on a coast.

    Boss: The Hush, Returned

    The Hush from the High Moor, now at home in the foundry and fed on its heat.
    HP 24,000
    Attack 225
    Defence 36
    Mind 214
    Speed 19
    XP / Marks 4,600 / 1,700
    Weak to Spark and Frost
    Resists nothing
    Drops Wind Draught (always)

    These are base figures. The game tunes bosses higher than the table shows, so expect a stiff fight. Train a few extra fights first; the Rust returns after a rest.

    Moves

    Move What it does
    Smother An ordinary hit on one hero. Its most common move
    Fogbank A magic blow on the whole party; about half the time it Dazes for 2 turns
    Stillness A weak magic hit on everyone, with a small chance to Seize for 1 turn
    Cinder Hush A magic hit on one hero, with a high chance of Rusting for 3 turns
    Gather It hurries itself so it acts sooner. No damage
    Silence BIG. A heavy blow on the whole party
    Not a Sound BIG. Its hardest single-target hit

    The moth's Glow warns you a turn before each BIG move. The boss follows a fixed 14-move cycle: it opens with Smother, Fogbank and Cinder Hush, Gathers and then Silences, and later lands Not a Sound. Not a Sound comes twice in each cycle, the second time as the very last move before it starts over.

    Advice. Both Spark and Frost are weaknesses, so bring Ottoline's Rime and Corwin's Arc. The Chains Thermal Shock and Cold Post are strong if your Bonds are high enough (see Bonds and chains). Ottoline's Read shows every move for the whole battle. Have Bett use Bulwark before Silence and Drop Anchor before Not a Sound. Poultice, Thaw, Oil Cans and Pepper Soup cure Rusting and Seizing. Bring Hot Broth and Lake Tonic, and rest at the Foundry's save clock first. More on tactics is in Battle basics.

    What the chapter leaves you with

    • The balloon, and a clear lead: the Engine is on the Hour Road.
    • The town running again, with Mrs Slade and Tam Cobb back on their feet.
