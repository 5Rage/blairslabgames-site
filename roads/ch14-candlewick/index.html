    Candlewick and the Chapel Road

    A lantern town on a wedding evening, where every lamp is lit, the guests are still waiting and a corner table teaches you the card game Cogs.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. At a glance
    2. Rust here
    3. Chests and secrets
    4. Dipper's Chandlery
    5. Cogs at the corner table
    6. Side stories and errands
    7. Hunt: the Runaway Cake
    8. Tips

    East of Saltglass the salt gives way to long grass lined with hundreds of lamps, every one lit and every flame the same height. Ahead is the town. You can hear a slow fiddle and a single bell ringing the same note over and over.

    Candlewick is dressed for a wedding, with bunting, long tables laid for the feast and guests who have clearly been standing about for a long time. This is also the chapter where you sit down at a corner table and learn to play Cogs.

    Note You can only come this way once the road east of Saltglass is open. Until then a line of parasols with a polite notice stands across it.

    At a glance
    Where The Lantern Fields (the approach), Candlewick (the square), the Wick and Wax (the inn), the Chapel Road, and the Chapel at Candlewick
    Who you can meet Master Snuff (Master of Ceremonies), Dame Maud Taper, Marigold Taper and Alder Wick, Barnaby Thistle (best man), Posy Hyssop (flower girl), Mistress Crumpet (baker), Pearl Dibble and her sister Opal, Verger Pennywhistle, Sexton Hollin, Constable Pike, and Aunty Ratchet with her card players
    Shops and inns Dipper's Chandlery, run by Mrs Dipper at the Wick and Wax. Goodman Snuffle keeps the inn. See Shops.
    Rest and save A camp on the west side of the square (it appears once you arrive), a bed at the Wick and Wax, and a save clock in the west vestry of the chapel
    Cogs Aunty Ratchet's corner table in the square, with players in the square and at the inn

    Rust here

    Ordinary Rust roam all the maps and are visible, so you can pick your fights. Full figures are on Larkhill to Saltglass Rust.

    Rust Weak to Notes
    Tallow Drip Frost Slow, waxy and sturdy. It resists Tide.
    Lantern Jack Tide Quick and fragile.
    Confetti Cannon Spark Hits the whole party with Mind attacks, and its confetti can cause Rusting. It sometimes drops a Wedding Cake Slice.

    Dame Guttering is an optional elite: a hardened Tallow Drip leading two Lantern Jacks, found once each in the Lantern Fields, on the Chapel Road and in the chapel. She is weak to Frost. Her Wax Over move slows the whole party, and the moth's Glow warns you a turn before she uses it.

    A boss waits at the end of this chapter. Bring a Spark or Gale user, plenty of healing and something to cure Rusting and Seized.

    Chests and secrets

    Where What
    Lantern Fields 2 Mulled Cider, 2 Wedding Cake Slice, and a hidden Buttonhole Rose (needs the moth's Seek)
    Candlewick 2 Wedding Cake Slice, 2 Plum Cordial, and a hidden 3 Oil Can (Seek)
    Chapel Road 2 Mulled Cider, 2 Wedding Cake Slice, 3 Plum Cordial, and a hidden 2 Frost Flask (Seek)
    The Chapel 2 Wedding Cake Slice, and a hidden 2 Plum Cordial (Seek)

    Use Seek in each map before you leave. The Buttonhole Rose in the Lantern Fields is worth the walk, and it is always cheaper than buying one.

    Dipper's Chandlery

    New gear a step above Saltglass's best:

    Item Marks Effect
    Wedding Sabre 3400 Attack +62, Speed +3
    Chime Rod 3400 Attack +14, Mind +65
    Candelabra Hook 3350 Attack +64, Defence +10
    Wick Glass 3350 Attack +9, Mind +63
    Morning Coat 3400 Defence +56
    Buttonhole Rose 450 Defence +2, Mind +2, Speed +2
    Wedding Cake Slice 130 Heals 400 HP
    Mulled Cider 160 Heals everyone 200 HP

    Tonics, Plum Cordials, Hot Broth, Oil Cans, Smelling Salts, Frost Flasks and Storm Jars are also on the shelves.

    Cogs at the corner table

    Aunty Ratchet teaches the rules of Cogs at the corner table in the square, and opens the ladder of players. The first four are here in Candlewick:

    Opponent Where First win pays
    Tuppence, the Tollgate Girl The square 15 Marks and a Tick Wheel card
    Mr Fenwick, the Clockmender The Wick and Wax 20 Marks and an Idler Gear card
    Nan Brassbottom, the Pie-shop Champion The square 30 Marks and a Jammed Verge card
    Piper Quill, the Stationmaster's Apprentice The Wick and Wax 40 Marks and a Wedding Bell card (rare)

    You must beat each opponent to challenge the next, so start with Tuppence. Only the first win over each pays out. Rust you have beaten sometimes drop a card too. The full rules and the cards are on Cogs and Cogs cards.

    Side stories and errands

    • The Dibble sisters' letters. Pearl Dibble in the square has not spoken to her sister Opal, at the inn, for a very long time. Carry Pearl's letter to Opal, bring Opal's reply back, and Pearl rewards you with 300 Marks and 2 Wedding Cake Slice.
    • The Verger's cider. Verger Pennywhistle, on the Chapel Road, is cold and would love a drop of Mulled Cider. Mrs Dipper sells it at the inn. Bring him one for 250 Marks and 2 Plum Cordial.

    Hunt: the Runaway Cake

    This chapter's Rust hunt begins at the notice board in the Wick and Wax. Read the poster, then follow the people it points to in the square, and the trail will lead you out into the Lantern Fields. It is a hard optional fight against a very large named foe that is weak to Tide and Gale, with a trinket and Marks for the winner. Do not take it on early. See Hunts.

    Tips

    • Bring Frost for Tallow Drips and Dame Guttering, Tide for Lantern Jacks, and Spark for Confetti Cannons.
    • Oil Cans cure Rusting and Seized, and both come at you from several Rust here.
    • Rest at the camp before the Chapel Road.
    • There is no shop on the Chapel Road, so go into the chapel with a full pack.

    The full story of this chapter is on Chapter 14 plot and bosses, with spoilers. Back the way you came: Saltglass.
