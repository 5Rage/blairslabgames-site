    Training and difficulty

    Why the bosses are hard, why most players lose the first time, and how a few extra fights and the difficulty setting fix it.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. Why bosses feel hard
    2. A few extra fights
    3. Learning a boss
    4. Difficulty settings
    5. Quick checklist before a boss

    The bosses in The Roads Between are hard. If you only fight what stands in your way, you will usually reach each boss a little under-levelled, and most players lose a boss fight the first time. That is intended, and a few extra fights fix it.

    Why bosses feel hard

    • Bosses are tuned so that a party which fights only what is on its path loses most first attempts.
    • Ordinary Rust are tuned too. A party at about the right level for a region takes a fair amount of damage from each group and loses an occasional fight. A party a couple of levels under loses more of them.
    • Heroes gain a small extra power bonus for every level above 10, so late levels matter more than they look.

    A few extra fights

    After about three more levels, which is roughly a dozen extra group fights, a boss becomes a fair fight. You don't need to grind for hours.

    Ways to train:

    1. Clear the region properly. Fight the Rust you walk past, not only the ones in the way.
    2. Rest, then fight again. Most Rust return after a rest, apart from those that roam close to a save clock or a camp.
    3. Take the optional demi-boss (elite) of each chapter. It is fought once and pays a large share of XP, Marks and drops.
    4. Spend your Mainspring points and keep your gear up to date at the local shop. See Growth and crafting.
    5. Bring the right element. A weakness makes a big difference. See Elements and statuses.

    Tip Losing is cheap. Try the battle again, watch what the boss does, and change one thing: bring a different element, heal sooner, or Guard when you see a BIG move coming.

    Learning a boss

    • Bosses follow a fixed pattern of moves rather than choosing at random, so you can learn them.
    • A move marked BIG is shown by the moth's Glow a turn before it lands. Guard, or keep a hero healthy enough to take it.
    • The moth's Seek shows weaknesses, and Ottoline's Read shows what every foe will do next.
    • You cannot flee from a boss.

    Difficulty settings

    Change it any time in Settings.

    Setting What it means
    Story Gentle battles: the Rust hits softer and falls faster. For people who want the story.
    Normal The standard setting. You will want to fight a few extra things along the way to keep up with each boss.
    Hard The Rust hits harder and takes longer to stop.

    If a boss will not budge, switching to Story for one fight is fine. The story doesn't change.

    Quick checklist before a boss

    • Rested at a bed, camp or save clock.
    • A few Tonics, Wind Draughts and an Oil Can or two in the bag.
    • The right element on hand: check the boss's weakness on Bosses and hunts if you do not mind spoilers.
    • Every hero's Mainspring points spent.
