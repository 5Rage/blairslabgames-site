    Hob

    The balloon pilot who becomes a Gale caster in Chapter 12 - fast, never misses, and always sorry about landing.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    Spoilers ahead.This page discusses story events and reveals. Come back after you have played if you want to find things out yourself.
    On this page
    1. At a glance
    2. How to play him
    3. Skills
    4. Stats
    5. Mainspring board

    Pilot of the Long Hope, the last balloon in the Evenwold. Gentle, quick, and a little afraid of landing. The wind does as he asks, and fliers cannot dodge it.

    Warning This page reveals that Hob Fenner becomes a hero in Chapter 12.

    Hob is the balloon pilot you meet earlier in the story, a gentle, quick man who is a little afraid of landing. As a hero he is a Gale caster: fast, with attacks that never miss, even against fliers.

    At a glance
    Role Balloon pilot
    Weapon kind bellows
    Joins Chapter 12
    Starting gear Pilot's Bellows, Pilot's Greatcoat.
    Chime The Long Hope
    Mainspring board Yes

    How to play him

    • Gust is his basic Gale attack (1.25x Mind) and never misses. Rust that sails or flaps hates it.
    • Tow takes a friend in tow: they go next, and the foe about to act is held back.
    • Updraft hits every foe with a wall of wind (0.85x Mind), with a 25 percent chance to Daze.
    • Cut the Ropes is one enormous gust (2x Mind) at the price of a slightly later next turn.
    • His Chime, The Long Hope, brings the whole balloon down the sky: Gale over every foe, and a shadow that slows them.
    • On the Mainspring, Aeronaut and Ground Crew.

    His Chains are Tailwind (with Wren: Gale on every foe, 1.5x Mind, never misses) and Waterspout (with Bett: Gale, 2.8x Attack, 80 percent chance to Daze, never misses).

    Skills

    Wind is what a skill costs. The Chime is a free limit move once the Chime meter is full (see Bonds, Chains and Chimes).
