    Weapons

    Every weapon you can find or buy, by kind, with bonuses, prices in Marks and where it turns up.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    A weapon can only be used by a hero of the matching kind: Wren uses blades, Corwin rods, Bett hooks, Ottoline lenses, Senn openers and Pip slings. Prices are in Marks; anything with a price can be sold back (key items cannot). Weapons and coats come from shops, chests, Rust drops and the smith.
