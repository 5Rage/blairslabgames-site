    Corwin

    A clockmaker thrown out of his guild - the Spark caster who delays foes and rewinds their last move.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. At a glance
    2. How to play him
    3. Skills
    4. Stats
    5. Mainspring board

    Thrown out of the clockmakers' guild for something he won't talk about. Can't leave a broken clock alone.

    Corwin is a clockmaker thrown out of the clockmakers' guild for something he won't talk about. In battle he is the party's Spark caster and time-bender: he deals damage, delays foes and can undo what a foe just did.

    At a glance
    Role Clockmaker
    Weapon kind rod
    Joins Chapter 1 (at level 2)
    Starting gear Tuning Rod, Travelling Coat.
    Chime Hour Hand
    Mainspring board Yes

    How to play him

    • Arc is his Spark attack and the best early answer to nearly any Rust (most early Rust are weak to Spark).
    • Delay pushes a foe back down the turn order. It is a cheap way to skip a BIG move.
    • Rewind (level 2) undoes the last thing a foe did, once a battle. It rolls back damage, statuses and the foe's place in the turn order.
    • Mend heals a friend for 2.4x Mind.
    • His Chime, Hour Hand, sweeps every foe with Spark and slows them.
    • On the Mainspring, Horologist boosts Spark and Repairer adds healing and durability.

    His Chains are Charged Delivery (with Wren), Steam Burst (with Bett) and Thermal Shock (with Ottoline).

    Skills

    Wind is what a skill costs. The Chime is a free limit move once the Chime meter is full (see Bonds, Chains and Chimes).

    Skill Learned Wind Targets Element Effect In the game's words
    Arc At the start 4 one foe Spark Hits for 1.3x Mind (Spark) A crackle of Spark from the tuning rod. The Rust hates it.
    Delay At the start 4 one foe plain pushes a foe back 0.7 of a turn Holds a foe's hands back: it moves down the turn order.
    Rewind Level 2 8 self plain undoes a foe's last action; replaced by Second Chance once learned Undoes the last thing a foe did. Once a battle.
    Mend Level 3 5 one friend plain heals 2.4x Mind Patches a friend up.
    Second Chance From the story 10 self plain undoes a foe's last action Rewind, reforged: undoes a foe's last action and steadies everyone. Twice a battle, and it works anywhere.
    Hour Hand Chime (limit move) 0 all foes Spark Hits for 2x Mind (Spark); 80% to inflict Slowed for 3 turns Corwin's Chime: Spark sweeps every foe and slows them.
