    The Roads Between

    Story RPG · AndroidWind the world's stopped clocks back up, one town at a time.

    Note The Roads Between is in closed testing and has not had a wide release. Everything on these pages describes the game as it stands today and may change before release, including numbers, names and what is in each chapter.

    What it is

    The Roads Between is a story RPG of about thirty to forty hours, set in the same world as the other Evenwold games. The great clocks of the Evenwold have stopped, the small towns have gone still, and the Rust (clockwork creatures gone to rust) has moved in.

    Wren, a courier's apprentice who has never been further than the next town, sets out with a clockmaker who walked out of his guild and a small moth made of soft golden light. They wind the world back up one town at a time. Every chapter ends with a town's clock running again, the people you met frozen mid-step finishing what they were doing, and the colour coming back.

    There are two parts. Part One runs from Chapter 1 to Chapter 10. Part Two begins with Chapter 11, and Chapters 11 to 14 are playable today.

    How it plays

    • You walk the world. Towns, roads and dungeons are maps you explore on foot. Doors take you between them, and a hidden chest or a sealed door is often the moth's cue.
    • The Rust is visible. Rust wanders the maps and notices you from a few tiles away. Touching one starts a fight. There are no random battles, so you choose what to take on. See Exploring and saving.
    • Battles are turn-based and readable. A turn-order bar shows who acts next, and you can bend it. You spend Wind on skills, fill a Chime for a limit move, and use Chains with friends you are close to. See Battle basics.
    • Bosses are hard on purpose. Most players lose a boss fight the first time unless they have trained a little. A few extra battles beforehand make it a fair fight, and losing costs you nothing. See Training and difficulty.
    • There is more than the road. You can craft, cook, fish, play a card game called Cogs, climb the ranks of the Arena, and track down optional hunts.

    • The party

      Every hero you can have in your party - who they are, how they play and where they join.

    • Corwin

    • Bett

    Chapters

    • Chapters and regions

      Every region you can visit so far, one page each, with what is new in each chapter and no spoilers.

    • Wicklow Halt and the Old Cutting

      The station village where you start, the road east of it, and a dark disused railway tunnel.

    • The Hopfields and the Wayward Light

      The hop gardens, a stilled canal lock, a windmill that thuds behind a barred door, and a lamp road that stays lit all night.

    • Tollbridge and Belcourt

      A town on both banks of a wide river with its toll gates shut, and a walled hill town that holds its wall every night.

    • Lowdelve and the Underway

      A mining valley of slate roofs and grey spoil heaps, and the pitch-black workings beneath it where Frost is king.

    • Saltmarsh Wharf

      A fog-bound harbour town where the tide has stopped all the way in, with two families next door to each other and a drowned quarter to drain.

    • The Valley and the Glasshouse Estate

      A long valley of stopped clocks leads to a walled estate where it is autumn on the lawns and summer behind the glass.

    • Hollowmere

      A still lake with a dry village at the bottom, a dam house on the shore and a road that lets you walk down.

    • The High Moor

      Snow, fog and a ring of standing stones on the roof of the Evenwold, with a shepherd's hut for shelter and a tower at the far side.

    • The Cinder Works

      A foundry town gone cold and silent, with a grounded balloon, a dark foundry full of heat-loving Rust, and a boss waiting at the far end.

    • The Hour Road

      A long, empty road east to the clock every other clock was set from, with a single house on the way, roaming Rust and three carved waystations.

    • Larkhill

      A green valley where it is always late afternoon, one long table that holds the whole village at supper, a cellar full of kitchen Rust, and the first cooking lessons.

    • Brindle Quay

      A bright, flag-hung harbour where the boats sit on the start line, and the place where you learn to fish.

    • Saltglass

      A white salt flat under a noon that will not end, with a Midsummer Fair, a shaded high street and the Saltglass Arena.

    • Candlewick and the Chapel Road

      A lantern town on a wedding evening, where every lamp is lit, the guests are still waiting and a corner table teaches you the card game Cogs.
