    Glossary

    Every term the game and this site use, from Almanac to Wind-up, in one list.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    Term Meaning
    Almanac (Clock Almanac) A menu page recording every kind of Rust you have met, and your fishing catch log.
    Arena The Saltglass Arena: an optional ladder of ten ranked duels. See The Saltglass Arena.
    Attack, Defence, Mind, Speed Hero and foe stats. Attack powers plain hits, Mind powers spells and healing, Defence reduces damage taken, Speed shortens the wait between turns.
    Bond How close two friends are. Built by camp talks, scenes and fighting together; five levels. Chains open at level 2. See Bonds, Chains and Chimes.
    BIG move A foe move that the moth's Glow shows a turn before it lands.
    Camp A place by the road to rest, save, and have a campfire talk.
    Campfire talk An optional chat between two friends at a camp. It builds their bond.
    Chain A move two friends do together; costs 4 Wind each.
    Chapter card The title screen that ends each chapter and names the next stop.
    Chime A hero's limit move, free once the Chime meter is full.
    Cogs Two things. (1) The card game Cogs, see Cogs, the card game. (2) Cogs (Quick Cog, Sturdy Cog and so on): small trinkets a smith makes from Rust Scrap and Marks.
    Dawn The moth's sixth Light.
    Duet A Chain with both Chimes full: free, stronger, spends both Chimes.
    Elite An optional demi-boss: a tougher version of one of a chapter's ordinary Rust, escorted by two of its smaller kin. Fought once.
    Escapement The part of a clock that lets it tick. Taking one out stops a clock.
    Evenwold The name of the world, used in the story.
    Exposed A status: the fighter takes about a third more damage.
    Gale The wind element, which never misses.
    Gear (element) Plain force: ordinary weapon hits.
    Glow, Seek, Kindle, Wake, Guide, Dawn The moth's six Lights. See The lantern moth and its Lights.
    Guard / Guarded Brace to take half damage until your next turn.
    Hunt An optional named Rust at the end of a clue chain. See Hunts.
    Kittiwake Bett's barge. It sails between moorings once the story frees it.
    Lights The lantern moth's abilities.
    Mainspring The skill boards: two branches of five nodes per main hero. See The Mainspring boards.
    Marks The money of the roads.
    Moth (lantern moth) A small creature of soft light that follows Wren. It never fights.
    Rewind Corwin's skill that undoes a foe's last action.
    Rust The clockwork creatures that have gone wrong. Also what you fight.
    Rust Scrap Crafting material left by every beaten Rust.
    Save clock A small clock face set in a wall where you can save and rest.
    Spark, Frost, Tide, Gale The four elements, besides plain Gear. See Elements and statuses.
    Wind The energy skills cost.
    Wind-up A small clockwork creature that appears as a friendly character on some maps.
