    Coats and trinkets

    Every coat and trinket, with bonuses, prices in Marks and where to find them.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    Each hero wears one coat and two trinkets (Menu, then Equip). Coats add Defence. Trinkets are small bonuses to Attack, Defence, Mind or Speed; many are the little Cogs a smith makes from Rust Scrap (see Growth and crafting), not to be confused with the Cogs card game.

    Coats

    Item Bonuses Price Where to get it Description
    Travelling Coat Defence +3 20 Wren starts with it; Corwin starts with it; Ottoline starts with it; Senn starts with it; Pip starts with it Warm, with a lot of pockets.
    Oilskin Coat Defence +6 110 Sold at Penhallow's Stores Rust slides right off.
    Canvas Jacket Defence +4 40 Bett starts with it Waxed canvas, smelling faintly of canal.
    Miller's Coat Defence +9 160 Chest in The Windmill; Sold at The Hop Exchange Thick, floury and surprisingly tough.
    Toll Coat Defence +13 300 Sold at Bridge Market A toll-keeper's greatcoat. Nothing gets through without paying.
    Pit Jacket Defence +17 470 Chest in The Underway (hidden, found with the moth's Seek); Sold at Cobb's Forge Leather, padded, and smelling of coal.
    Storm Coat Defence +22 700 Chest in The Sunken Quarter; Sold at The Chandlery Waxed, double-stitched, and it has never once let the sea in.
    Gardener's Coat Defence +27 980 Chest in The Great Glasshouse; Sold at The Manor Kitchen Thornproof moleskin with deep pockets full of string.
    Waterman's Coat Defence +32 1300 Chest in Hollowmere; Sold at The Dam House Oiled wool, cork-lined. Floats, if it comes to it.
    Fleece Coat Defence +38 1750 Chest in The High Moor; Chest in The Hour Road; Sold at The Shepherd's Hut Thick moorland fleece. Nothing gets through, not even the cold.
    Harvest Smock Defence +43 2300 Chest in The Rolling Downs; Sold at Gammon's Grocer Heavy linen with deep pockets, and a surprising number of crumbs in them.
    Regatta Blazer Defence +49 2900 Sold at Cleat's Chandlery Striped, stiff with salt, and smart enough for a race day. Weatherproof. Lined with signal flags.
    Saltweave Coat Defence +50 3000 Sold at Sallow's Sundries White linen stiff with salt. Stands up by itself and keeps the sun off.
    Duellist's Longcoat Defence +50 - Found in the world Rank six: Captain Gnash's spare. Sand-proof, drum-proof and extremely long.
    Morning Coat Defence +56 3400 Sold at Dipper's Chandlery Dove-grey wool, tails, a silk lining. Nothing sticks to it, not even a thirty-year-old stain.

    Landing Ring Defence +4, Speed +3 - Story reward A chalk circle, rubbed onto a brass ring by the quay children. Hob wears it for luck. Landings are going better.
    Buoy Bell Defence +3, Speed +4 - Story reward The clapper off Old Clanger. It still rings, but only when it means to, and kindly.
    Winner's Rosette Attack +3, Mind +3, Speed +3 - Story reward Blue silk, gold lettering: FIRST. The Quay's, and now yours, for the one race that finally started.
    Wide Straw Hat Defence +2, Speed +2 400 Chest in Saltglass Fair (hidden, found with the moth's Seek); Sold at Sallow's Sundries Halves the Fair's heat pulses for whoever wears it. Looks wonderful on everybody.

    Tin Laurel Defence +4, Mind +3 - Found in the world Rank two in the Saltglass Arena. Dented, and worn with great pride.
    Escapement Loupe Mind +8, Speed +2 - Found in the world Rank eight: Madam Chime's. You can see exactly when to stop.
    Champion's Sash Attack +5, Defence +5, Speed +4 - Found in the world Rank ten: the Noon Champion's gold sash. It casts no shadow either.
    Buttonhole Rose Defence +2, Mind +2, Speed +2 450 Chest in The Lantern Fields (hidden, found with the moth's Seek); Sold at Dipper's Chandlery A real rose, wired to a brass pin, that never wilts. Everyone at Candlewick wears one.
    Cake Topper Attack +3, Defence +3, Mind +3 - Story reward Off the top tier of the Runaway Cake: two sugar figures holding hands. They are exactly the right size for a pocket.
    Wedding Favour Attack +3, Mind +3, Speed +3 - Story reward A silver ribbon knotted round three sugared almonds. From Marigold and Alder, who finally got to give them out.
