    Growth and crafting

    Levels, XP, the Mainspring boards, gear and what the smith can make from Rust Scrap.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. Levels and XP
    2. Mainspring boards
    3. Gear
    4. Crafting with Rust Scrap
    5. The Almanac

    Levels and XP

    • Heroes start at level 1 (new heroes join at a set level, or at the party leader's level) and can reach level 60.
    • Every battle gives XP to the whole party. The XP needed for the next level grows steadily: level 1 to 2 needs 10 XP, level 10 to 11 needs 282, level 30 to 31 needs 1,386 and level 59 to 60 needs 3,696.
    • A hero who is knocked out still gets half the XP, and a hero behind the party's highest level catches up faster, so a hero who keeps falling does not drift further behind.
    • A level-up raises HP, Wind, Attack, Defence, Mind and Speed by the hero's own growth rates, and may teach a new skill. Skill levels are on each hero's page under The party. A few skills are taught by the story instead.
    • Heroes also gain a small power bonus for every level above 10.

    The game is tuned so that fighting only what stands in your way leaves you a little short at each boss, and a few extra fights close the gap. See Training and difficulty.

    Mainspring boards

    Each of the four main heroes has a Mainspring board (Menu, then Mainspring): two branches, each with five nodes. A hero earns one point per level after the first and spends them on nodes, which cost 1 to 3 points. Nodes in a branch are bought in order, and you may pick freely between branches.

    • Wren's Courier branch makes her faster and gives her a first turn that comes early; her Guardian of the Post branch makes her sturdier.
    • Corwin's branches are Horologist (Spark) and Repairer (healing and durability).
    • Bett's are Bargewoman and Tidewater, and Ottoline's are Natural Philosopher (Frost) and Field Physician.
    • Nodes give stats or a passive: a first turn that comes early, 2 Wind back every turn, starting each fight Guarded, a chance to strike back, harder hits for one element, a faster Chime, or more critical hits.
    • Resetting a board costs 25 Marks, so you can try a different path.
    • Guest heroes have no board.

    Every node is listed on The Mainspring boards.

    Gear

    Each hero wears one weapon, one coat and two trinkets (Menu, then Equip).

    • A weapon must match the hero's weapon kind: Wren uses blades, Corwin rods, Bett hooks, Ottoline lenses, Senn openers, Pip slings.
    • Gear adds to Attack, Defence, Mind or Speed. Weapons and coats come from shops, chests, Rust drops and the smith.
    • Trinkets are mostly the small Cogs that a smith makes. These are not the Cogs card game.
    • Everything with a price can be sold. Key items cannot.
    • Full lists: Weapons, Coats and trinkets, Shops.

    Crafting with Rust Scrap

    Every beaten Rust leaves Rust Scrap (bigger Rust leave more; bosses leave a fixed amount). From Chapter 4 the smith at Cobb's Forge in Lowdelve has a Make page that turns Scrap plus Marks into things you cannot buy:

    Makes Rust Scrap Marks Effect
    Quick Cog 6 60 Speed +3
    Sturdy Cog 6 60 Defence +4
    Keen Cog 6 60 Attack +3, Speed +1
    Bright Cog 6 60 Mind +4
    Frost Flask (makes 2) 3 20 Deals 60 Frost damage to a foe
    Master Cog 18 200 +2 to every stat

    Tip Each hero has two trinket slots, so a pair of Cogs on everyone is a cheap boost before a boss.

    The Almanac

    The Clock Almanac (a menu page) records every kind of Rust you have met. A kind is recorded the first time you fight it. See the Bestiary.
