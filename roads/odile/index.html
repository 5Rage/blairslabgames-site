    Odile Marr

    A retired lamplighter who joins as a guest in Chapter 14 - heals, steadies and cures.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    Spoilers ahead.This page discusses story events and reveals. Come back after you have played if you want to find things out yourself.
    On this page
    1. At a glance
    2. How to play her
    3. Skills
    4. Stats

    Warning This page reveals that Odile Marr joins the party in Chapter 14.

    Odile Marr, a retired lamplighter with forty years of dusks behind her. Calm, dry, kind. Her lamp heals, steadies and shows the Rust where it stands.

    Odile Marr is a retired lamplighter. She joins in Chapter 14 as a guest hero, which means no Mainspring board. In battle she is the party's support: she heals, shields and cures.

    At a glance
    Role Lamplighter
    Weapon kind pole
    Joins Chapter 14 (guest)
    Starting gear Lamp Pole, Lamplighter's Coat.
    Chime The Last Lamp
    Mainspring board No (a guest)

    How to play her

    • Snuffer is a hit with the lamp pole's snuffer cap, with a 35 percent chance to Daze.
    • Lamp Glow heals one friend (2.6x Mind) and cures Rusting and Seized.
    • Steady Flame (level 3) guards every friend for a turn. Lantern Light (level 5) heals everyone a little and cures Rusting and Seized.
    • Her Chime, The Last Lamp, goes lamp to lamp down the line: everyone is healed (2.2x Mind) and cleared of Rusting and Seized.

    Skills

    Wind is what a skill costs. The Chime is a free limit move once the Chime meter is full (see Bonds, Chains and Chimes).

    Skill Learned Wind Targets Element Effect In the game's words
    Snuffer At the start 3 one foe plain Hits for 1x Mind; 35% to inflict Dazed for 2 turns The lamp-pole's snuffer cap, brought down on a foe's light. May daze it.
    Lamp Glow At the start 5 one friend plain heals 2.6x Mind; cures Rusting and Seized Holds the lamp up to a friend: heals them and cures Rusting and Seized.
    Steady Flame Level 3 7 all friends plain 100% to inflict Guarded for 1 turn A flame that does not flicker: every friend is guarded until their next turn.
    Lantern Light Level 5 9 all friends plain heals 1.2x Mind; cures Rusting and Seized Lights the whole road: heals everyone a little and cures Rusting and Seized.
    The Last Lamp Chime (limit move) 0 all friends plain heals 2.2x Mind; cures Rusting and Seized Odile's Chime: she goes lamp to lamp down the line, and everyone is back on their feet.
