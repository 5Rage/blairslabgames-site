    Chapter 8 plot and boss - The Shepherd's Clock

    The fog splits the party in two, the moth learns to guide, and the Hush is driven off the standing stones.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    Spoilers ahead.This page discusses story events and reveals. Come back after you have played if you want to find things out yourself.
    On this page
    1. What happens
    2. Party and moth
    3. Boss: The Hush
    4. What the chapter leaves you with

    Warning This page gives away the plot and the boss of this chapter.

    Back to the place guide: The High Moor. Previous: Chapter 7 plot. Next: Chapter 9 plot.

    What happens

    The rail-cart climbs onto the High Moor: snow, wind, fog, and a ring of standing stones with a clock set into one of them. Pip Tallow is waiting at the halt. His gran sent him up the line with the moor post and told him, "If you find them, stick with them this time, you great lump." She has also made him a new sling from belfry rope, the Steeple Sling. Pip joins the party.

    Shepherd Ewart says nobody has been up to the stones since the fog came down. Something is in it, and sheep, who go near everything, will not go near. He also talks fondly of the tower down the moor and its lamp.

    When the party heads for the stones, the fog comes off the high ground all at once, and the quiet comes with it. The wind and the snow stop, and Wren cannot hear her own feet. Something taller than the stones passes through and snowflakes hang still where it goes. Corwin names it: the Hush, which Aldous built to keep things quiet. He shouts for everyone to stay together, and the fog closes in. Wren and Pip are alone.

    The West Fold. Wren and Pip cannot hear or find the others. The moth glows brighter and flies a little way ahead, then waits, and gains a new Light, Guide. Wren says it has been doing that since the very first morning. They come out at the foot of a tower so tall its top is lost in cloud, with hundreds of small clockwork insects around its base and a lamp burning far above. Wren sends the moth back into the fog for the others.

    The East Fold. Bett, Corwin and Ottoline are lost in the dark too. Bett offers to carry Ottoline out. Ottoline says she can read the Rust in the dark and Bett cannot, and tells her to hold on to her, for once. After a pause, Bett agrees: "All right. Lead." Their Bond jumps, and their Chain move Ice Bulwark now guards everyone. The moth's small light bobs out of the fog and they follow it.

    At the tower's foot the two halves meet again. Bett says Ottoline got them out. Ottoline says Bett let her lead. Corwin says the Hush has gone back up to the stones to guard the clock, as Aldous always left things guarded.

    Party and moth

    • Pip joins as a fifth hero. Only four fight at a time, so change who fights in the Menu, Party.
    • The moth gains Guide (Light 5). It leads the way through fog and, in battle, shows what every foe will do next.
    • Bett and Ottoline's Bond grows. Their Chain move Ice Bulwark now guards everyone at once.

    Boss: The Hush

    At the standing stones the fog stands up and becomes the Hush, as tall as the stones. Wren says they are mending this clock together.

    These are base figures. The game tunes bosses higher, so expect a stiff fight.
    HP 20000
    Attack 150
    Defence 32
    Mind 144
    Speed 18
    XP and Marks 3800 XP, 1400 Marks
    Weak to Spark
    Resists Nothing
    Drops Wind Draught (always)

    Moves in plain words

    • Smother: a plain hit on one hero.
    • Fogbank: a Mind attack on everyone with a good chance of Dazing them.
    • Stillness: a light Mind attack on everyone that can leave them Seized.
    • Muffle: a light hit that pushes the target back in the turn order.
    • Gather: it hurries itself.
    • Silence (BIG): a heavy hit on the whole party.
    • Not a Sound (BIG): its heaviest hit on a single hero.

    The moth's Glow shows both BIG moves a turn early, and Guide shows every move.

    The pattern. It repeats a fixed fourteen-move cycle: Smother, Fogbank, Muffle, Smother, Gather, Silence, Stillness, Smother, Not a Sound, Fogbank, Smother, Muffle, Silence, Not a Sound. Silence lands on the sixth and thirteenth moves and Not a Sound on the ninth and last, so save Bett's Bulwark and your healing for those turns.

    Advice. Spark is the element to bring: Corwin's Arc, Storm Jars (80 Spark damage, 60 Marks at the Shepherd's Hut), and Pip's Flare, which hits every foe with Spark and can Daze them. Pip's Long Shot never misses. Corwin's Second Chance (twice per battle) is best kept for the worst moves. Bring cures for Seized and Rusting (Oil Cans, Ottoline's Poultice and Thaw, the moth's Wake) and plenty of healing, such as Hot Broth, which heals the whole party, and Lake Tonic. It has more health and Mind than the Keepsake, the toughest boss so far, so train a few extra fights first. The Rust returns after a rest.

    What the chapter leaves you with

    After the fight the Hush draws back into itself and slides away down the far side of the moor. It is not beaten, just gone for now. Corwin says it is going home, to the Cinder Works, to him. You will meet it again in Chapter 9.

    Pip scrambles up the stone, since it is the highest thing around, and Corwin passes up the tools one by one. The moth circles the shepherd's clock and its hands move. The fog lifts from the whole moor at once, and wind, snow and every sheep sound off together.

    In a crack of the stone is another note: "The stones stood here before any clock. Let them stand quiet again. - S." Pip says, "So that's him. The one doing all this." Corwin says that's him, and the next clock is his own: the Cinder Works, where the engine is. The rail-cart line now runs on there from Moor Halt. Next: The Cinder Works.
