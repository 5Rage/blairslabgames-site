    Shops, inns and the smith

    Where to spend your Marks - every shop and what it sells, plus inn and smith prices.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page

    2. The Hop Exchange (Chapter 2)
    3. Bridge Market (Chapter 3)

    Resting at an inn costs 10 Marks and heals the whole party. Resetting a Mainspring board costs 25 Marks. Everything you can sell fetches a share of its price. Every shop stocks the basics (Tonic, Wind Draught, Oil Can and so on), and each region adds its own gear, so check the shop in each new town before you spend your Marks elsewhere.
