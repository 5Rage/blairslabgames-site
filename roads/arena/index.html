    The Saltglass Arena

    Ten ranked clockwork duels against fighters with names like Sir Highsun, with a reward for every win and no cost for losing.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. How it works
    2. The ladder
    3. The duellists

    The Arena opens in Chapter 13, at Saltglass. It is an optional ladder of ten ranked clockwork duels. You never have to enter and the story doesn't need it, but it tests a party well and pays in Marks and gear you can't buy.

    How it works

    • The Arena opens once you have spoken to Marshal Brass, and the rank board lists the ten fighters.
    • Rank 1 is open to you at once. To challenge rank n, you must have beaten ranks 1 to n minus 1.
    • Each rank is a hard duel against a named fighter. Use any party you like.
    • The first win of a rank pays once: Marks, and at every second rank a piece of gear that cannot be bought.
    • A beaten rank can be fought again for a small purse, but only the first three rematches of each rank pay anything. After that it is for honour.
    • Duellists give no XP, Marks, Rust Scrap or drops, so the Arena cannot be farmed.
    • Losing costs nothing. The game restores the moment before the fight, and you can try again.
    • The level shown below is only a guide to when a steady party finds the rank a fair fight. You can challenge a rank at any level.

    The duellists

    Figures are base stats before the game's scaling. Each duellist has a plain strike, a party-wide Mind attack with a status chance, and one BIG move that the moth's Glow shows a turn early.

    Rank Opponent HP Attack Defence Mind Speed Weak to Plain strike, party attack, BIG move
    1 Tin Tolliver: a tin soldier in a very large helmet 2,115 68 28 48 15 Spark Bayonet, Salute (Dazes), Charge!
    2 Mags Mangle: a laundry-mangle with arms 2,925 76 31 55 16 Frost Wring, Squeeze (Slows), Full Cycle
    3 Lord Tick: a pocket-watch nobleman who fences by the second 2,075 85 34 63 17 Tide Lunge, Stop the Watch (Seizes), Quarter Hour
    4 Brisk Bridget: a quick automaton, 25% Evade 1,449 94 38 71 18 Spark Flicker, Dazzle (Dazes), Flurry
    5 The Salt Duchess: a porcelain lady with a salt cellar for a crown 1,989 103 41 79 19 Tide Parasol Tip, Pinch of Salt (Rusts), Royal Seasoning
    6 Captain Gnash: a brass-toothed bruiser with a drum for a belly 1,791 113 44 88 20 Frost Gnash, War Drum (Slows), Broadside
    7 Old Hollis Hardhand: a retired champion, rusted and unbothered 1,683 124 47 98 21 Spark Hardhand, Old Grip (Slows), Last Word
    8 Madam Chime: a bell on legs 2,423 134 50 107 22 Frost Clapper, Toll (Seizes), Grand Peal
    9 The Glass Knight: very hard to hit, 20% Evade 1,420 146 54 118 23 Tide Glass Edge, Mirror Guard (Dazes), Shatter
    10 Sir Highsun: the Noon Champion, gold armour and no shadow 2,446 157 57 128 24 Frost Highsun Blade, Blinding Noon (Dazes), Zenith

    An Arena Hand (a hired clockwork second who tidies the sand and hits anyone who stands on it) turns up too: 300 HP, weak to Spark, with a sand toss and a party-wide rake.

    Tip Each duellist is weak to one element, and Frost, Spark and Tide are all in the ladder's rotation. Bring a party that can hit all three, and keep Guard for the BIG move.
