    Rust of Larkhill, Brindle Quay, Saltglass and Candlewick

    Every ordinary Rust met between Chapters 11 and 14, with stats, weaknesses and drops.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. Roaming Rust
      1. What they are like
    2. Smaller kin and optional demi-bosses

    These are base figures. The game scales Rust up with the difficulty you choose and tunes each chapter and boss on top, so the numbers are only good for comparing one Rust with another. Evade is the chance a hit misses. BIG moves are the ones the moth's Glow warns about a turn early.

    Bosses and hunts are on Bosses and hunts (with spoilers). The index is the Bestiary.

    What they are like

    • Ladle-Walker. A copper soup ladle on stilt legs, striding about with a bowl of hot gravy that never empties. Frost sets the gravy. Moves: Scoop (power 1.1); Hot Gravy (power 0.8 (Mind), hits the whole party, 40% Slowed (3 turns), BIG).
    • Runaway Pie Tin. A round tin that got out of the oven and kept going. Fast, hard to catch, and it crashes into things. Spark rattles it. Moves: Roll Over (power 1); Crust Crash (power 1.9, BIG).
    • Bobbing Buoy. A bell-buoy that got off its chain and bobs about on dry land, ringing at everything. Spark rattles its works; Tide runs straight off it. Moves: Bob and Bump (power 1.1); Ring Out (power 0.8 (Mind), hits the whole party, 50% Dazed (2 turns), BIG).
    • Anchor Hound. A ship's anchor with a chain for a tail, bounding along the boards like a very heavy dog. Frost stiffens the chain. Wind just rolls off it. Moves: Chain Whip (power 1.15); Drag the Bottom (power 0.9, hits the whole party, 45% Slowed (2 turns), BIG).
    • Salt-Cellar Crab. A squat brass salt cellar on six crab legs that shakes itself over anything hurt. A splash of Tide dissolves it. Moves: Pinch (power 1.1); Salt in the Wound (power 0.7 (Mind), hits the whole party, 40% Rusting (3 turns), BIG).
    • Gnomon Sentry. A tall automaton with a sundial for a face and a long pole for an arm. It throws its shadow at you, and the shadow is heavy. Frost cools its gears. Moves: Pole Poke (power 1.2); Long Shadow (power 0.8 (Mind), hits the whole party, 50% Slowed (2 turns), BIG).
    • Tallow Drip. A slow heap of candle wax on six candelabra legs, forever dripping. What it drips over goes stiff and slow. Cold makes the wax brittle. Moves: Drip (power 1.1); Wax Over (power 0.7 (Mind), hits the whole party, 50% Slowed (2 turns), BIG).
    • Confetti Cannon. A brass party cannon on cart wheels that fires rice and paper gears at the whole line. Rusting confetti gets everywhere. A jolt of Spark sets it off early. Moves: Rice Shot (power 1.2); Confetti Burst (power 0.75 (Mind), hits the whole party, 40% Rusting (3 turns), BIG).

    Smaller kin and optional demi-bosses

    These Rust walk with the optional demi-boss (an elite) of each chapter, a tougher version of one of the chapter's ordinary Rust, escorted by two smaller kin. An elite is fought once and does not come back after a rest, and it pays a large share of XP, Marks and drops.

    • Pepper-Mill Hornet. A pepper mill with wings, buzzing round your head and sneezing at you. A splash of Tide washes the pepper off. Moves: Pepper Sting (power 1 (Mind), 30% Dazed (2 turns)); Pepper Cloud (power 0.8 (Mind), hits the whole party, 50% Dazed (2 turns), BIG).
    • Flapping Jib. A loose sail that has torn off its boom and is having the time of its life. Quick, and it flaps out of the way. Wind spins it right round. Moves: Whip (power 1); Luff (power 0.7 (Mind), hits the whole party, 40% Dazed (2 turns), BIG).
    • Heliograph Hare. A long-eared hare made of polished mirrors, flashing signals to nobody in particular. Its glint dazes. A jolt of Spark overloads the mirrors. Moves: Hind Kick (power 1); Glint (power 0.8 (Mind), hits the whole party, 50% Dazed (2 turns), BIG).
    • Lantern Jack. A street lantern that walked off its post on a pair of stilt legs, swinging its light into everyone's eyes. Quick on its feet. A splash of Tide douses it. Moves: Pole Swing (power 1); Glare (power 0.8 (Mind), hits the whole party, 50% Dazed (2 turns), BIG).
    • Head Cook. A hardened ladle-walker. It comes with two smaller kin. Moves: Scoop (power 1.1); Hot Gravy (power 0.8 (Mind), hits the whole party, 40% Slowed (3 turns), BIG).
    • Commodore Cleat. A hardened anchor hound. It comes with two smaller kin. Moves: Chain Whip (power 1.15); Drag the Bottom (power 0.9, hits the whole party, 45% Slowed (2 turns), BIG).
    • Sergeant Shadowless. A hardened gnomon sentry. It comes with two smaller kin. Moves: Pole Poke (power 1.2); Long Shadow (power 0.8 (Mind), hits the whole party, 50% Slowed (2 turns), BIG).
    • Dame Guttering. A hardened tallow drip. It comes with two smaller kin. Moves: Drip (power 1.1); Wax Over (power 0.7 (Mind), hits the whole party, 50% Slowed (2 turns), BIG).
