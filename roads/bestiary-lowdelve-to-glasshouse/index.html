    Rust of Lowdelve, the Wharf and the Glasshouse

    Every ordinary Rust met between Chapters 4 and 6, with stats, weaknesses and drops.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. Roaming Rust
      1. What they are like
    2. Smaller kin and optional demi-bosses

    These are base figures. The game scales Rust up with the difficulty you choose and tunes each chapter and boss on top, so the numbers are only good for comparing one Rust with another. Evade is the chance a hit misses. BIG moves are the ones the moth's Glow warns about a turn early.

    What they are like

    • Rust Tunneller. A boring machine that never stopped boring. Plated nose; Spark finds the motor. Moves: Drill (power 1.15); Bore In (power 2, BIG).
    • Firedamp. A drifting glow of mine gas with a rusted spark inside. Spark only feeds it; Frost puts it out. Moves: Flicker (power 0.9 (Mind)); Flashover (power 0.8 (Mind), hits the whole party, 30% Dazed (2 turns), BIG).
    • Rust Tub. A coal tub that rolls itself along the rails. Water rusts its wheels solid. Moves: Ram (power 1.1); Runaway Tub (power 1.9, BIG).
    • Pit Boiler. A pumping boiler, red-hot and walking. Spark just warms it up; Frost cracks it. Moves: Scald (power 1 (Mind), 40% Rusting (3 turns)); Blow Off (power 0.75 (Mind), hits the whole party, BIG).
    • Rust Crab. A dock crane's grab that walks sideways now. Thick shell; Frost makes it brittle. Moves: Pinch (power 1.15); Clamp Shut (power 1, 50% Seized (1 turn), BIG).
    • Rust Eel. A coil of rusted chain that swims. Quick and slippery; Spark through the water stops it dead. Moves: Lash (power 1); Coil (power 1.6, 50% Seized (1 turn), BIG).
    • Bell Buoy. A channel buoy with a cracked bell inside. Its toll slows everything down. Spark rings it hollow. Moves: Clang (power 0.9 (Mind), 30% Dazed (2 turns)); Toll the Deep (power 0.85 (Mind), hits the whole party, 50% Slowed (3 turns), BIG).
    • Trawl Net. A fishing net full of rusted floats, hauling itself along. Anything it catches is stuck fast. Frost stiffens it. Moves: Haul (power 1.1); Cast Net (power 0.6, hits the whole party, 35% Seized (1 turn), BIG).
    • Ticker. A Rust-taken wind-up from the valley's workshops, ticking toward the bell. Spark stops its spring. Moves: Tick (power 1); Wound Strike (power 1.8, BIG).
    • Plateback. A heavy wind-up behind a plate of iron. Spark finds the seams. Moves: Shove (power 1.15); Plate Slam (power 2, BIG).
    • Glider. A wind-up on paper wings. Hard to reach; Frost weighs it down. Moves: Swoop (power 1); Dive (power 1.8, BIG).
    • Rust Topiary. A clipped hedge-peacock on a clockwork frame, gone wild. Frost wilts it. Moves: Snip (power 1.1); Overgrow (power 0.8 (Mind), hits the whole party, 50% Slowed (3 turns), BIG).
    • Rust Sprinkler. A garden sprinkler spinning rust-water everywhere. Shrugs off Tide; Spark shorts it. Moves: Spray (power 0.9 (Mind), 40% Rusting (3 turns)); Downpour (power 0.8 (Mind), hits the whole party, 40% Rusting (3 turns), BIG).
    • Rust Mower. A lawn roller with blades, mowing nothing in neat stripes. Water jams its drum. Moves: Mow (power 1.15); Cut a Swathe (power 1, hits the whole party, BIG).

    Smaller kin and optional demi-bosses

    These Rust walk with the optional demi-boss (an elite) of each chapter, a tougher version of one of the chapter's ordinary Rust, escorted by two smaller kin. An elite is fought once and does not come back after a rest, and it pays a large share of XP, Marks and drops.

    • Tin Canary. A miner's clockwork canary, rusted and shrieking. Quick, and hard to hit. Moves: Peck (power 0.9); Shrill (power 0.4 (Mind), hits the whole party, 50% Dazed (2 turns), BIG).
    • Rust Gull. A weathervane gull that flew off its roof. Hard to hit; Frost weighs its wings down. Moves: Peck (power 0.95); Dive Bomb (power 1.8, BIG).
    • Scuttler. A quick little wind-up, all legs. Hard to hit; Frost slows its gears. Moves: Scuttle (power 0.95); Scramble (power 0, speeds itself up).
    • Old Boiler. A hardened pit boiler. It comes with two smaller kin. Moves: Scald (power 1 (Mind), 40% Rusting (3 turns)); Blow Off (power 0.75 (Mind), hits the whole party, BIG).
    • Hulk Trawl. A hardened trawl net. It comes with two smaller kin. Moves: Haul (power 1.1); Cast Net (power 0.6, hits the whole party, 35% Seized (1 turn), BIG).
    • Head Groundskeeper. A hardened rust mower. It comes with two smaller kin. Moves: Mow (power 1.15); Cut a Swathe (power 1, hits the whole party, BIG).
