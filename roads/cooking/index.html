    Cooking

    Meals, recipes and ingredients - what heals, what buffs the whole party for the next fight, and where the ingredients come from.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. How it works
    2. Recipes
    3. Where ingredients come from

    Cooking begins in Chapter 11 (Larkhill), where Mrs Crumb shows the party how it is done, and grows in Chapter 12 with recipes built around fish.

    How it works

    • A recipe turns ingredients (ordinary items: crops, fish, Rust drops) into a meal, which is also an ordinary item.
    • You can cook a recipe once you know it. The first three recipes come from Mrs Crumb. Others are taught by side stories, such as Nan Marsh's duff.
    • A meal either heals (eaten from the bag or in battle) or gives the whole party a buff for the next battle. A buff meal is eaten outside battle and lasts until the next fight.

    Note At the moment you learn recipes and receive meals from people, and meals can be eaten from the bag, but the cooking pot at camps is not open yet.

    Recipes

    Recipe Makes Ingredients Effect
    Harvest Stew 1 2 Downs Mushroom, 1 Butter Crock, 1 Flour Sack Heals one friend 330 HP.
    Apple Pie 1 2 Larkhill Apple, 1 Flour Sack, 1 Butter Crock, 1 Honey Jar Heals everyone 200 HP.
    Pepper Soup 2 2 Pepper Pods, 1 Downs Mushroom, 1 Flour Sack Heals 140 HP, restores 25 Wind, cures Rusting and Seized.
    Honey Cake 1 2 Honey Jar, 1 Flour Sack, 1 Butter Crock Whole party hits 12 percent harder in the next battle.
    Marsh Duff 1 2 Larkhill Apple, 2 Flour Sack, 1 Honey Jar, 1 Butter Crock Heals everyone 120 HP and raises Defence by 15 percent for the next battle.
    Peppered Pasty 2 1 Pepper Pods, 1 Downs Mushroom, 1 Flour Sack, 1 Butter Crock Whole party is 12 percent quicker in the next battle.
    Sailor's Chowder 2 2 Quay Sprat, 1 Butter Crock, 1 Flour Sack Heals everyone 230 HP.
    Grilled Herring 2 2 Silver Herring, 1 Pepper Pods Whole party is 10 percent quicker in the next battle.
    Fisherman's Pie 1 1 Cobble Flounder, 2 Downs Mushroom, 1 Flour Sack, 1 Butter Crock Heals everyone 210 HP and raises Defence by 12 percent for the next battle.
    Gale Skate Skewers 2 1 Gale Skate, 1 Pepper Pods, 1 Honey Jar Whole party hits 14 percent harder in the next battle.
    Admiral's Feast 1 The Old Admiral, 1 Regatta Bass, 1 Honey Jar, 1 Flour Sack Heals everyone 320 HP and raises Mind by 15 percent for the next battle.

    Where ingredients come from

    Ingredient Where
    Downs Mushroom, Butter Crock, Flour Sack Gammon's Grocer in Larkhill, Cleat's Chandlery at Brindle Quay, and some story rewards
    Larkhill Apple Gammon's Grocer; dropped by the Runaway Pie Tin (30%)
    Honey Jar Gammon's Grocer, Cleat's Chandlery, a chest on the Rolling Downs, a chest in Sailmakers' Row; dropped by the Ladle-Walker and the Head Cook (25% each)
    Pepper Pods Gammon's Grocer, Cleat's Chandlery, story rewards; dropped by the Pepper-Mill Hornet (30%)
    Fish (Quay Sprat, Silver Herring, Cobble Flounder, Gale Skate, Regatta Bass, The Old Admiral) Fishing at Brindle Quay and the Regatta Course

    The meals themselves are listed under Consumables.
