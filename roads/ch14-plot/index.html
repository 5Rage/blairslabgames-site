    Chapter 14 plot and boss - The Wedding Bells

    The party helps a bride finally reach her chapel after thirty years, meets a retired lamplighter, and faces a peal of bells that cannot find its last note.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    Spoilers ahead.This page discusses story events and reveals. Come back after you have played if you want to find things out yourself.
    On this page
    1. What happens
    2. Party and moth
    3. Boss: The Wedding Bells
    4. Hunt: The Runaway Cake
    5. What the chapter leaves you with

    Warning This page gives away the plot and the boss of this chapter.

    What happens

    You step out of the Fair's east gate into the Lantern Fields, and the first thing Bett notices is that the music smells like a wedding. Master Snuff, the Master of Ceremonies, greets the party in the square with great warmth and no sense of time. The bride left her house at dusk, the groom went out to meet her, and everyone has been waiting for them to reach the chapel ever since. It is still dusk, thirty years later.

    Snuff explains that the Wedding Bells, a peal of bell-machines in the chapel belfry, ring the bride in: one toll for every step. Nobody has told them what to do when she arrives. Sabine Averill, a retired lamplighter and guest of the family, explains the rest. Not one lamp in town has gone out since the wedding day, and a day cannot end while all its lamps are lit. She would like to put out the three lamps on the chapel porch, which hold the door latched, but the road is thick with Rust and her snuffer-pole is not a weapon. She joins you.

    Dame Maud Taper, the bride's mother, has been holding her daughter's bouquet for thirty years. The roses have gone gold at the edges and never wilted. You carry them east along the Chapel Road to Marigold Taper, who walks with her groom Alder Wick one very small step to each toll. If she steps early, the road grows longer. The last time she counted four hundred and twelve steps, it turned out to be four hundred and thirteen. Marigold admits what she fears most: arriving, and finding that the next day is only an ordinary Tuesday. Alder says he would very much like that Tuesday, and a sandwich on a bench. She asks you to stop the Bells, not break them.

    At the end of the road Sabine snuffs the three porch lamps, in any order, and the chapel door unlatches. Inside, three bell-ropes on the walls (north-west, north-east and a small one in the east vestry) wake the clockwork and lift the brass gate across the chancel steps.

    Behind the gate the ceiling is hundreds of bells hung in a swinging lattice around a round brass dial. Ottoline sees what is wrong: the Rust has gathered round the one note it cannot make, like dust round a stopped clock. Wren asks it nicely, and you fight.

    When the Bells fall quiet, the lattice swings in one wide arc and every bell rings at once, and then once more, softly, to the last bell on the left. That is the last peal the machine was missing. You step onto the porch into a different evening, deep blue and full of stars. Marigold comes up the road at a happy, slightly too-fast step with her mother's roses in her fist, shouting that she counted four hundred and twelve (Alder says thirteen). The wedding finally happens, the Bells ring one clear note for the last step, and Sabine goes down the road snuffing lamps while the town lights up window by window. There is a first dance on the cobbles. Even Corwin, who insists he does not dance, does.

    Party and moth

    • Sabine Averill joins as a guest, with 40 years of dusks behind her. She is a calm, dry, kind support hero with a Lamp Pole and a Lamplighter's Coat that are hers alone. Her skills: Snuffer (a light hit that may daze), Lamp Glow (heals one friend and cures Rusting and Seized), Steady Flame (guards everyone), Lantern Light (heals the party a little and cures) and her Chime, The Last Lamp (heals and cures everyone). She has no Mainspring board, and she waits in reserve until you bring her forward from the Party menu. See Sabine.
    • Bonds grow between Sabine and each of the four, strongest with Wren and Bett.
    • The moth gains no new Light in this chapter.

    Boss: The Wedding Bells

    These are base figures. The game tunes bosses higher, so expect a stiff fight.
    HP 38,700
    Attack 228
    Defence 47
    Mind 220
    Speed 23
    XP 9,300
    Marks 3,900
    Weak to Spark, Gale
    Resists Frost
    Drops Wind Draught (always)

    Moves

    Move What it does
    Clapper Swing A solid single hit
    Wedding Toll Mind hit on the whole party, may Seize for a turn
    Discord Mind hit on the whole party, likely to Daze for two turns
    Rice Rain Mind hit on one friend, very likely to cause Rusting
    Ring the Changes Hurries the Bells themselves
    Full Peal (BIG) Hard hit on the whole party
    Here Comes the Bride (BIG) A massive hit on one friend

    The moth's Glow warns you a turn before each BIG move, so guard or heal ahead of it.

    The pattern. It opens with Clapper Swing, Discord and Rice Rain, then Clapper Swing, Ring the Changes, Full Peal and Wedding Toll. After another Clapper Swing it uses Here Comes the Bride, then Discord, Clapper Swing, Rice Rain, Full Peal and Here Comes the Bride again, and the cycle repeats. Two BIG moves in the closing stretch, back to back, are the dangerous part.

    Advice. Bring Spark and Gale users, since both hit it hard and Frost is wasted. Keep Oil Cans and Sabine's Lamp Glow ready for Rusting and Seized, and have Sabine's Steady Flame up before Full Peal. Fully heal after Here Comes the Bride and keep the wounded friend out of its way. This is a stiff fight, so train a few extra fights first, the Rust returns after a rest. Dame Guttering and the Dibble errands are worth finishing beforehand for the healing items.

    Hunt: The Runaway Cake

    The notice board at the Wick and Wax, Mistress Crumpet in the square, and Barnaby Thistle the best man point you to a trail of crumbs in the far south-east of the Lantern Fields. The cake is frightened of being cut. Hunts has more.
    HP 37,000
    Attack 345
    Defence 44
    Mind 325
    Speed 27
    XP 3,500
    Marks 1,000
    Weak to Tide, Gale
    Resists Frost
    Drops Mulled Cider (always)

    Its BIG moves are Icing Barrage (the whole party) and Top Tier Topple (one friend). Sugar Storm Dazes the party, Marzipan Cling can Seize the party and Jam Spit usually causes Rusting. Winning earns the Cake Topper trinket (Attack, Defence and Mind +3) and 400 Marks from the story.

    What the chapter leaves you with

    • 1,900 Marks, 3 Mulled Cider and the Wedding Favour trinket (Attack, Mind and Speed +3) from the happy couple.
    • Sabine in your party, and the Cogs ladder open at the corner table.
