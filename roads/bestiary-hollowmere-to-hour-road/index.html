    Rust of Hollowmere, the Moor and the Hour Road

    Every ordinary Rust met between Chapters 7 and 10, with stats, weaknesses and drops.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. Roaming Rust
      1. What they are like
    2. Smaller kin and optional demi-bosses

    These are base figures. The game scales Rust up with the difficulty you choose and tunes each chapter and boss on top, so the numbers are only good for comparing one Rust with another. Evade is the chance a hit misses. BIG moves are the ones the moth's Glow warns about a turn early.

    Bosses and hunts are on Bosses and hunts (with spoilers). The index is the Bestiary.

    What they are like

    • Silt Walker. A drowned field-engine, walking out of the lake mud. Thick with silt; Spark gets through. Moves: Trudge (power 1.15); Mudslide (power 0.9, hits the whole party, 50% Slowed (3 turns), BIG).
    • Rust Pike. A long iron fish that swims through the held air as if it were water. Spark through it, quick. Moves: Snap (power 1.1); Ambush (power 1.9, BIG).
    • Rust Sexton. The chapel's bell-ringing automaton, still ringing for a village that left. Its toll holds you still. Moves: Knell (power 0.9 (Mind), 30% Dazed (2 turns)); Toll for the Drowned (power 0.85 (Mind), hits the whole party, 30% Seized (1 turn), BIG).
    • Rust Ram. A weathervane ram off a moor farm, charging at anything that moves. Spark rattles its horns. Moves: Butt (power 1.1); Charge (power 2, BIG).
    • Rust Cairn. A pile of moor stones and old gears that stood up one night. Water washes its footing away. Moves: Topple (power 1.15); Rockfall (power 1, hits the whole party, BIG).
    • Scale Beetle. Rust from the foot of the great tower, as the wind-ups there know it: armoured, slow, stubborn. Spark finds the joints. Moves: Ram (power 1.15); Plate Crush (power 1.9, BIG).

    Smaller kin and optional demi-bosses

    These Rust walk with the optional demi-boss (an elite) of each chapter, a tougher version of one of the chapter's ordinary Rust, escorted by two smaller kin. An elite is fought once and does not come back after a rest, and it pays a large share of XP, Marks and drops.

    • Rust Heron. A weathervane heron that walks the shallows, spearing at anything. Quick, and Frost stiffens its neck. Moves: Stab (power 1); Spear Dive (power 1.8, BIG).
    • Hush-Wisp. A scrap of the fog that goes before the Hush. It whispers, and you forget what you were doing. Spark scatters it. Moves: Whisper (power 0.9 (Mind), 35% Dazed (2 turns)); Hush (power 0.8 (Mind), hits the whole party, 30% Seized (1 turn), BIG).
    • Burr Spider. Tower Rust with burred legs that catch on everything. Frost makes it brittle. Moves: Bite (power 1.05); Web (power 0.7 (Mind), hits the whole party, 50% Slowed (3 turns), BIG).
    • Pitted Wasp. Tower Rust on rattling wings. Quick and hard to hit; Frost slows its wings. Moves: Sting (power 1, 30% Rusting (3 turns)); Dive (power 1.8, BIG).
    • Lakebed Walker. A hardened silt walker. It comes with two smaller kin. Moves: Trudge (power 1.15); Mudslide (power 0.9, hits the whole party, 50% Slowed (3 turns), BIG).
    • The Old Cairn. A hardened rust cairn. It comes with two smaller kin. Moves: Topple (power 1.15); Rockfall (power 1, hits the whole party, BIG).
    • Furnace Scarab. A hardened scale beetle. It comes with two smaller kin. Moves: Ram (power 1.15); Plate Crush (power 1.9, BIG).
    • Hour Ram. A hardened rust ram. It comes with two smaller kin. Moves: Butt (power 1.1); Charge (power 2, BIG).
