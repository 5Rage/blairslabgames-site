    Helpers and automation

    Five helpers you earn by climbing, each one a switch that takes over a chore while you play.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. The five helpers
    2. Good to know
    3. Curio hunt
    4. Using helpers well

    As the hive climbs it earns helpers: small automatic hands that take over the busywork. Each helper is earned once, by reaching its floor in any Era, and is kept for good through every Rewind and the Grand Winding. They cost nothing to run.

    Every helper is a switch. Let it run, or turn it off and do it all by hand.

    The five helpers

    Helper Earned at floor What it does
    Store-keeper 15 Builds a Cistern, Bin or Rack when that store is 90% full
    Comb-builder 30 Builds the housing that gives the most room for its price, when it costs under a quarter of what you hold
    Archivist 40 Studies the cheapest research when it costs under a quarter of your Schematics
    Chart-reader 50 Sends the swarm into the chamber that holds your next pattern while you climb, when you have the Gearshards
    Pattern-fitter 70 Applies a found pattern and levels the piece back up, once your Springs can make it at least as strong as before

    Good to know

    • Helpers only work while the game is open. During time away or a skip, they wait. That way you do not come back to stores that were spent as they filled.
    • They never spend what a gift gave you. A resource held over its storage limit, such as from a gift pack, is left alone.
    • Helpers and research are separate. Helpers are earned once for good; Standing Orders and Self-fitting Equipment are researches bought again each run (Standing Orders stays learned once you have it). See Build and research.

    Curio hunt

    The Chart-reader has an extra switch of its own: Curio hunt. With it on, the Chart-reader waits until your swarm has gone a minute without clearing a cog, then runs the highest chamber your Gearshards can pay for that is at least two levels below your wall. Easier chambers can still hold curios, and a hunt chamber that stalls too is left. Chambers 20 or more levels below hold no curios, so below that it waits. It is a hands-off way to look for curios when the climb has stalled.

    Using helpers well

    • With the Store-keeper on, you rarely need to watch storage.
    • The Comb-builder and Archivist buy things only when the price is under a quarter of what you hold.
    • The Pattern-fitter saves the tapping needed to level a piece back up after every pattern.

    Tip Turn a helper off when you are saving up for something specific and flip it back on afterwards.
