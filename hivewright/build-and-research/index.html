    Build and research

    Every building and research in the hive, what it does and when it appears.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. How prices grow
    2. Buildings
      1. Storage
    3. Research
      1. Tapping research
    4. Standing Orders and Self-fitting Equipment
    5. What to build first

    The Build tab holds your buildings, sorted into four groups: Housing, Workshops, Storage and The Climb. The Research tab holds schematics, paid for in Schematics, sorted into five groups. Both tabs show a new entry only when you are able to use it.

    How prices grow

    Every building and research costs more each time you buy it. A Cell's price, for instance, grows 25% with every Cell you own. The table shows the starting price and how fast it rises per copy owned. Storage buildings are different, see below.

    Buildings

    Building What it does First price Grows by Needs
    Cell Houses 3 wind-ups 10 Brass 25% Nothing
    Comb Houses 7 120 Brass, 80 Oil, 60 Springs 30% Comb Patterns
    Gallery Houses 18 1,000 Brass, 600 Oil, 600 Springs 30% Gallery Patterns
    Spire Houses 35 8 million Brass, 5 million Oil, 5 million Springs 100% (doubles) Spire Patterns, floor 40
    Hatchery Assembles wind-ups 40 Oil, 30 Brass 60% Nothing
    Offering Altar +5% to everything wind-ups gather 150 Oil, 120 Brass 35% Altar Rites
    Proving Ground +10% swarm attack and health 250 Brass, 150 Oil, 200 Springs 50% Swarm Drill, floor 4
    Hoardcomb Raises Oil, Brass and Springs limits together (x1.1 each) 800 Oil, 800 Brass, 400 Springs 60% Comb Patterns, floor 5
    Escapement Hall Swarms strike 3% more often for each one 1,500 Brass, 900 Springs, 900 Oil 90% Swarm Drill, floor 50

    Storage

    The Oil Cistern, Brass Bin and Spring Rack raise the limit on their resource. Each one costs 60% of the limit it is about to raise, so the next store always asks for most of a full store, and the limit and the price climb together. The Spring Rack needs Coil Winding.

    When a store is full, gathering of that resource stops and nothing is wasted. Schematics and Gearshards have no limit.

    Tapping research

    Research What it does Needs
    Nimble Claws +1 to each tap (up to 10 levels) Nothing
    Borrowed Hands Each tap also gathers what your workers bring in over 5% of a second, per level Nimble Claws
    Moth's Favour A tap on the resource the moth sits on gathers double, and one more time per further level Nimble Claws
    Overflowing Hands Taps can fill Oil, Brass and Springs 5% past the limit, per level Borrowed Hands
    Winding Touch Every gathering tap also winds the great key a little Swarm Drill

    Researches with several levels cost more with every level. The multi-level ones start at about double per level, and the strongest tapping boosts climb much more steeply.

    Note Research and buildings are bought again each run, since a Rewind resets them. The exception is Standing Orders: once you have researched it, it starts every later run already learned.

    Standing Orders and Self-fitting Equipment

    These two are the hive's automation research.

    • Standing Orders lets idle wind-ups take jobs by themselves, following the split you choose, so the hive keeps growing while you are away.
    • Self-fitting Equipment buys the cheapest equipment level whenever it costs under a tenth of your Springs. It waits while your Springs are over the storage limit, so Springs you got from a gift stay yours to spend.

    Each is a switch you can turn off.

    What to build first

    1. A Cell, then a Hatchery.
    2. Oil Cistern and Brass Bin as soon as stores hit their limit.
    3. Comb Patterns and a Comb.
    4. A Proving Ground from floor 4, and an Offering Altar for steady extra gathering.
    5. Hoardcomb from floor 5 for bigger storage.

    See Tips for fast progress for ordering that pays off over several runs.
