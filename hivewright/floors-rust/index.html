    Floors and the Rust

    How tough the Rust gets floor by floor, what it is called and what each floor pays.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. The Rust creatures
    2. How the numbers grow
    3. What each floor pays
    4. Drops
    5. Landmarks at a glance

    The Rust gets thicker the higher you climb, a flaky orange-brown scale over the brass, with creatures hiding in the shadows.

    The Rust creatures

    Ordinary Rust creatures come in seven kinds, and the kind you meet depends on the floor and cog:

    • Rust mite
    • Flake crawler
    • Scale beetle
    • Burr spider
    • Verdigris slug
    • Pitted wasp
    • Corroded weevil

    They all use the same stats for a given floor and cog. The name is only for flavour. The last cog of each floor holds the Gatekeeper, see Gatekeepers and landmarks.

    How the numbers grow

    The Rust on the first cog of each floor has these base stats:

    Floor Rust health Rust attack
    1 130 8
    5 about 370 about 21
    10 about 1,400 about 69
    20 about 19,000 about 750
    30 about 260,000 about 8,200
    40 about 3.6 million about 89,000
    50 about 50 million about 980,000

    The rules behind the table:

    • Health grows 30% per floor. Attack grows 27% per floor.
    • Each cog on a floor is a little tougher than the last: +2% per cog, so the 49th cog is nearly double the first.
    • The first five floors are eased to help new players: the Rust on floors 1 to 5 has 50%, 60%, 70%, 80% and 90% of its normal health. The Gatekeepers of floors 1 to 4 are also lighter.
    • A chamber of a given level uses the Rust stats of that floor level.
    • Floors you have already climbed are remembered. Floors more than ten below your best in the current Era are weaker, the further down the easier, so the early part of every run after a Rewind is quick.

    Your swarm grows faster than this early on, then you hit walls. See Tips for fast progress.

    What each floor pays

    Clearing a floor pays about a minute of your hive's gathering, with a minimum of 15 times the floor number in each resource. The Music-box comb curio and some Salvage choices raise or lower floor rewards.

    Drops

    • Gearshards come from the Gatekeeper of every floor and from the Rust on every tenth cog.
    • Salvage comes from the Gatekeeper of every tenth floor. See Salvage.
    • Story fragments are told on each landmark floor.

    Landmarks at a glance

    Floor What opens or happens
    1 First Gatekeeper
    2 Tuners and Stingers
    3 Formation Drills and the Carapace
    4 Proving Ground
    5 Wings, Hoardcomb, and a first-run gift
    10 First story fragment, first Salvage choice, expeditions
    15 Store-keeper helper, a first-run gift
    20 Rewind
    30 Comb-builder helper, Standing Orders
    40 Archivist helper, Spire
    50 Chart-reader helper, Escapement Hall
    60 Self-fitting Equipment
    70 Pattern-fitter helper
    100 The first great clock
