    Tips for fast progress

    Practical habits for early runs, stalls, Rewinds and long idle stretches.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. In your first run
    2. Keep the key wound
    3. When a swarm keeps falling
    4. Rewinding well
    5. Idle well
    6. Taps stay useful

    In your first run

    1. Follow the moth's goals in order. They are written so each one unlocks the next thing you need. See Getting started.
    2. Put every wind-up to work. An idle wind-up gathers nothing (although each idle one speeds up the Hatchery a little).
    3. Keep homes ahead of the Hatchery. The hive says when it is full.
    4. Raise storage the moment a store fills. A full store stops gathering. See Build and research.
    5. Start the Climb early. Researching Swarm Drill costs only 40 Schematics, and floors 1 to 5 are eased for new players.

    Keep the key wound

    The key sets work and attack between 75% and 100%. Tap Wind when you check in, or give about one in ten wind-ups the Winder job. See Wind-ups, jobs and the great key.

    When a swarm keeps falling

    1. Level your cheapest equipment first. Each level adds to every wind-up in the swarm.
    2. Research Formation Drills for bigger swarms, and build a Proving Ground once floor 4 opens it.
    3. Raise Hatchery speed so a fallen swarm returns quickly.
    4. Pick a Salvage card that covers your weak side, such as health if swarms die fast. See Salvage.
    5. Chase expedition patterns: a tier is a x2.3 jump in a piece's strength. See Equipment and expeditions.
    6. If you have reached floor 20, think about a Rewind.

    Rewinding well

    • Do not Rewind the moment you can. Aether grows 9% for each extra floor after 20, so a few more floors are worth a great deal. A good time to Rewind is when the next floors are taking much longer than the last ones.
    • Use the Aether preview. The Rewind tab tells you how many perk levels the Aether would buy.
    • Start with Deft Gathering and Sharp Swarm, the cheapest perks. Add Warm Hatchery and Head Start as runs get longer.
    • Try a Trial early. After two Rewinds, a Trial's permanent Aether bonus pays back every later Rewind. Silent Key unlocks the Taut Key perk. See Trials.

    Idle well

    • Let helpers do the chores. Climb to floors 15, 30, 40, 50 and 70 to earn them. See Helpers and automation.
    • Research Standing Orders at floor 30 so idle wind-ups take jobs for you.
    • Buy Long Rest if you leave the hive for long stretches, or take the Night Shift Salvage when you know you are heading away.
    • Save Time Jumps for a stall. A jump is worked out like time away, so it is worth more the stronger your hive is. See Offline progress and optional boosts.

    Taps stay useful

    Tapping research (Nimble Claws, Borrowed Hands, Moth's Favour, Overflowing Hands and Winding Touch) keeps manual play worthwhile. Tap the resource where the lantern moth has settled for the most.

    Tip If a run goes badly after your first Rewind, you can start it over for nothing before reaching floor 20. Perks and records are kept.
