    The Grand Winding and Eras

    The deepest reset, which starts a new Era with richer rewards, a tougher tower, a new Harness and a new colour for the hive.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    Spoilers ahead.This page discusses story events and reveals. Come back after you have played if you want to find things out yourself.

    Warning This page describes the late game in full.

    The Grand Winding is a deeper reset above the Rewind. It opens when you restart the first great clock by climbing past floor 100. It is optional, and it is always previewed: the confirmation screen lists exactly what resets, what stays and what you gain before you commit.

    What an Era is

    The game counts Eras. Your first playthrough is Era I. Each Grand Winding begins the next Era: II, III, IV and so on, without end.

    The Grand Winding opens again at higher floors as you go. You must climb past 100 floors in Era I, 200 in Era II, 300 in Era III, and so on. Each Era asks for 100 more floors than the last.

    What resets

    Everything earned in play: all resources, Aether and every perk, buildings, jobs, research, equipment, expeditions, Salvage, Trial progress and stars, your floor, and the Loft.

    What stays

    • Curios, which are kept for good.
    • The lantern moth.
    • Helpers, which are earned for good.
    • Your Era count and every theme you have unlocked.
    • Time Jumps in your stored pile.
    • Records: the game keeps your best floor in every Era, shown in the Journal.
    • Anything you bought in the Shop, and your achievements.

    What you gain

    • A permanent Era reward: everything wind-ups gather, and your Aether gains, are multiplied by 2 for each Era. Era II is x2, Era III x4, Era IV x8.
    • A theme for the hive. Each Era unlocks a new metal for the tower and the hive. Era II unlocks Silverwork, then Goldwork, Obsidian and Starlight. They are earned only by playing. Once unlocked, a theme can be used in any Era.
    • A new Harness (see below).
    • A new opening line as the tower wakes. Era II begins: "Silver light runs down the stair. Somewhere above, a second great clock is waiting to be found."

    What gets harder

    Each Era raises:

    • Rust and Gatekeeper health and attack: x3 per Era. Era II is x3, Era III x9, Era IV x27.
    • Building and equipment prices: x1.5 per Era.
    • Landmark Gatekeepers gain one extra ability per Era: Mending, Plated, Twinned and Furious (see Gatekeepers and landmarks).
    • The Rust gets a new name: Tarnished, then Gilt-rot, Glassbound and Starved.

    Rewards double while the Rust triples, so the early floors go faster at first, then the walls arrive sooner. To get through them you need better perks, smarter Salvage and Trial rewards.

    The Harness

    Every Era past the first adds one Harness, named after its theme: Silverwork Harness in Era II, Goldwork Harness in Era III and so on. You keep the earlier ones.

    • Each level of a Harness multiplies swarm attack and health by 1.06.
    • A Harness is paid for in Springs, starting at 50,000 for Era II's. Each later Era's Harness costs 50 times more, and climbs in price faster per level.
    • It resets on a Rewind, so you build it up again each run.
    • It is the growth that lets a later Era climb its extra hundred floors.

    Tip The game's own advice after a Grand Winding is to level the new Era's Harness in the Climb tab. It multiplies the whole swarm's strength, so it is worth building up again each run.

    Era-only Trials and Salvage

    Some Trials and Salvage cards belong to later Eras:

    Trial Opens in Rule Stars at floor
    Bare Harness Era II No Harness can be levelled 60 / 90 / 120
    Slow Hatch Era II The Hatchery works at half speed 70 / 105 / 140
    Thin Shelves Era III Oil, Brass and Springs storage is halved 110 / 165 / 220

    Their Aether bonuses are 25%, 10% and 15%. Three Salvage cards, Deep Combs, Overwound Key and Patient Stores, are left only by the Gatekeepers of Era II and later.

    Achievements

    Three optional Play Games achievements belong here: The Grand Winding for beginning a new Era, Harnessed for levelling a Harness, and The second great clock for climbing past floor 200.

    Should I do it?

    Do it when you feel ready. Nothing in the shop can skip or speed it up.
