    The Loft

    A second layer above the hive where you share the great key's spare winding among six gear trains that level up by themselves.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    Spoilers ahead.This page discusses story events and reveals. Come back after you have played if you want to find things out yourself.
    On this page
    1. The six trains
    2. Torque and power
    3. How it fits with everything else

    Warning The Loft opens at floor 60. This page describes it in full.

    Above the hive, a loft opens once you have reached floor 60 in any Era. In the Loft, the great key's spare winding is stored as Torque. You share that Torque out among six gear trains. Each train turns by itself, slowly, even while you are away. Every time one comes all the way round, it gains a level: a small, lasting boost to part of the hive. Later levels take longer.

    Aether has a second use here besides perks: it buys more Torque and more power.

    The six trains

    Train Boosts Per level
    Gathering Train Everything wind-ups gather +1%
    Hatching Train Hatchery speed +1%
    Striking Train Swarm attack +1%
    Plating Train Swarm health +1%
    Aether Train Aether from Rewinds +0.5%
    Shard Train Gearshard drops +1%

    Each level compounds on the last, like perks.

    Torque and power

    • You start with 10 Torque to share. Each capacity upgrade, bought with Aether, adds 5 more.
    • Each train turns at a rate set by the Torque on it, times your power. Each power upgrade, bought with Aether, multiplies the speed by 1.25.
    • The first level of a train takes 5,000 Torque-seconds. Each later level needs 35% more than the one before.
    • Capacity costs 100 Aether for the first upgrade and rises 60% each time; power costs 150 and rises 80% each time.
    • Share evenly splits all your Torque between the six trains in one tap.

    Tip Torque on a train is not spent: you can move it from one train to another whenever you like. Put it on Aether before a Rewind you are about to do, and on Gathering or Striking while you push through a wall.

    How it fits with everything else

    The Loft is kept through Rewinds, like perks. It starts again at the Grand Winding, with perks and Aether. Trains turn while you are away like everything else, and a long absence can bring several levels at once.

    Read about the deeper reset on The Grand Winding, and about the permanent boosts you earn along the way in Rewind and perks.
