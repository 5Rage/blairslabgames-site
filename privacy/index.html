    Privacy and cookies

    What this website does and does not collect, how ads and cookies work if they are switched on, and how to reach us.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    This page covers the website at blairslabgames.co.nz. Our mobile games have their own privacy policies (linked from the game's store page and, where available, from the footer of this site), and those are the ones that apply when you play.

    At the moment this site shows no ads and sets no advertising cookies.

    What we collect on this site

    • No accounts, no sign-up, no forms. You can read everything without telling us who you are.
    • No analytics. We do not use analytics or tracking tools on this site.
    • Your theme choice. If you press the light/dark toggle, your browser stores that choice (in "local storage") so the next page matches. It never leaves your device and we cannot see it.
    • Server logs. The site is a plain set of static files served by our hosting provider. Like any web host, the provider may keep ordinary technical logs, such as your IP address, the page requested and the time, for security and operation. We do not use these logs to identify or follow individual visitors.
    • Search. The search box runs entirely in your browser. What you type is not sent anywhere.

    Ads and cookies

    We plan to fund the games, in part, with ads on this site from Google AdSense. Ads never block or gate content. Read more on the ads page.

    If and when ads are switched on:

    • Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads and to measure how they perform. They may use your device's identifiers and your general location.
    • Personalised ads use data such as your browsing and what you do on other sites. Non-personalised ads use only the page you are on and your approximate location.
    • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, ad cookies will only be set after you agree through a consent banner, and you will be able to change your mind at any time using the "Ad choices" link in the footer.
    • How to opt out of personalised ads: visit Google's Ad Settings, or aboutads.info (US) or youronlinechoices.eu (Europe). You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Doing so does not stop non-personalised ads.
    • To learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see Google's explanation.

    We do not sell any information about you. We do not receive your personal browsing data from Google or its partners.

    Children

    Our games and this website are for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you think a child has sent us information, tell us and we will delete it.

    Other sites

    Pages here link to other websites, including Google Play and Discord. They have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for them.

    Your rights

    Because we hold no personal information about visitors to this site, there is usually nothing for us to give you or delete. If you have a question, think we hold something about you, or want to ask us to correct or delete it, contact us and we will help. Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you rights over your information.

    Changes

    If we change how this site handles information, we will update this page and its date. This page describes our practices in plain language; it is not legal advice.
