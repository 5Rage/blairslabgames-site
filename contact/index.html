    Contact

    How to reach BlairsLab Games for support, feedback, bug reports and press.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. Email

    We are a small team and we read every message.

    Email

    support@blairslabgames.co.nz is the best place for support questions, bug reports, feedback, press and anything about this website.

    For a bug report, it helps to tell us:

    1. Which game you were playing.
    2. What kind of phone or tablet you have.
    3. What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.

    Discord

    Come and chat with other players, share what you have built and see what is coming next on our Discord.
