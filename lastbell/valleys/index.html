    The valleys

    Wandering Roads, nine hand-made valleys and the Open Field: how each is unlocked and what makes it different.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. Wandering Roads
    2. Hand-made valleys
    3. The Open Field
    4. Picking a valley
    5. Water and trees
    6. Twists

    A valley is the ground you defend, with its roads, pads and scenery. There are three kinds.

    Wandering Roads

    The everyday valley. It begins every run with two roads, and they wind a new way every run, so you have to make a slightly different plan each time. Most players spend most of their time here.

    Hand-made valleys

    Nine hand-made valleys have fixed roads and their own ground and scenery. Each opens when your best wave reaches a set number:

    Valley Opens at best wave Layout
    The Crossroads 15 Four short roads from every side like a pinwheel
    Switchback Hill 25 One long road zig-zagging up the heath
    The Spiral 35 A single road coiling round the tower
    The Mill Race 45 A stream crossed by two plank bridges, plus a road from the west
    Orchard Rows 55 Two long straight rows between apple trees
    The Ring Road 65 One great road that laps the bell twice
    The Forked Brook 75 A brook splits in two, with a road along each bank
    The Narrows 85 Two long straight lanes between hedges
    Market Square 95 Four short lanes spilling into a crowded square

    The full guide is on Hand-made valleys.

    The Open Field

    No roads at all: you build the maze. It opens after your first recast. See The Open Field.

    Picking a valley

    After a few runs, the Foundry's start screen offers Wandering Roads, the featured monthly valley, any hand-made valleys you have unlocked, and Trials. Wandering Roads is always available.

    Water and trees

    Water and trees are tiles nothing can be built on. A road that crosses water is a bridge, as on the Mill Race and the Forked Brook.

    Twists

    Any valley can be played with a twist: the monthly valley, the Daily Valley and the Peal all add them. The featured monthly valley is always one of the first five hand-made valleys.

    Tip Hand-made valleys are the same every run, so they are a good way to learn where the best pads are.
