    Rewind the minute

    How the bell rings backwards: the 30-second window, echoes, Hindsight, charges and the bell that rewinds itself.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. What happens when you ring back
    2. The minute plays the same way
    3. Hindsight
    4. Charges
    5. The bell keeps watch
    6. The moth remembers
    7. When to ring back

    Tamsin Hale's notebook says that if you ring the bell the wrong way it remembers the last minute and lets you have it again. It also says the bell tires, so you should let it gather itself between times.

    What happens when you ring back

    Tap Ring back 30 s. The last 30 seconds of the run are undone: wind-ups, Parts, towers and the mainspring all return to how they were half a minute ago.

    Then the field pauses, and the erased minute stays behind as faint echoes:

    • Glowing trails show the paths of wind-ups that got through to the bell.
    • Towers ringed in grey never struck anything in that minute.

    While the field is paused you may:

    • Move towers,
    • Sell towers for a full refund (outside a rewind, selling returns 70 percent),
    • Build or upgrade with the Parts you had at that moment.

    When you are ready, tap Play the minute again.

    The minute plays the same way

    The wind-ups come in the same order and the same way. That flock of Gliders will come over the grass again, and that Plateback will be on the road again, so whatever you change is what makes the difference.

    Hindsight

    Each time you replay a stretch, your towers strike 10 percent harder until you pass the moment you rang back from, and this stacks up to +30 percent if you ring back repeatedly. So rewinding helps even if you change nothing, which is handy when you are playing hands-off.

    Charges
    Starting charges 1
    Most the bell can hold 3 (with two Foundry upgrades)
    Recharge A charge comes back after 40 mends
    Faster recharge Foundry "Quick bell" takes 5 mends off per level; research "Swifter echoes" takes 2 off per level, down to a minimum of 10

    Mends in a replayed minute are not counted twice, so you can't farm charges by rewinding.

    The Foundry's upgrade The bell remembers lets the bell hold one more charge, twice. See Recast, Foundry and stars for the costs.

    The bell keeps watch

    Another Foundry upgrade, The bell keeps watch, lets the bell act on its own: when the mainspring would run down and the bell has a charge, it rings back by itself and plays the minute again. It helps a lot when you leave a run playing.

    The moth remembers

    A rewind doesn't move the lantern moth. It stays exactly where it was.

    When to ring back

    • Early is the cheapest time to learn. Your first rewind teaches you to read echoes.
    • Save the charge for a wave you did not predict, such as the first Gliders, the first Warden of an hour or a Toll.
    • Read the echoes. If trails cluster on one road, you don't have enough coverage there. If a tower is ringed in grey, move it.
    • Chain rewinds when you have two or more charges: the Hindsight bonus stacks.

    Some Trials take the bell away completely or slow its recharge. See also Tips for deep waves.

    Note In the Story, the keeper rewinds at the bell tower because it is the last ticking clock in the valley.
