    Research and time away

    Six research projects that keep working while the game is closed, and what the bell gathers while you are away.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. The tower holds
    2. Research
    3. Time skips
    4. Away earnings
    5. Daily tasks

    The tower holds

    When you leave the game, the wave doesn't move, so you never come back to a lost run. Research keeps going and Bell-metal trickles in.

    Research

    Open the Foundry, pick a project and pay its Bell-metal. It then takes real time (minutes at first, then hours) and keeps going whether the game is open or not. You have one bench, and the Foundry's A second bench gives you two.

    Project Effect per level Levels First level costs First level takes
    Finer springs Every tower strikes 6 percent harder 20 60 Bell-metal 5 min
    Quicker hands 8 percent more Parts from every mend 15 50 5 min
    Deeper spring The mainspring holds 10 percent more 15 40 4 min
    Swifter echoes A rewind charge comes back 2 mends sooner 6 120 15 min
    Longer reach Every tower reaches 3 percent further 8 150 20 min
    A patient bell Bell-metal gathers for one more hour while you are away 8 100 2 h

    Each level costs more and takes longer than the last: costs rise by 60 percent per level for the first three projects and by 90 to 120 percent for the last three, and times grow in a similar way.

    Finished research strengthens every run from the next one on.

    Time skips

    Some daily tasks and Trial stars give time skips: minutes taken off the project on a bench. If no project is running, the time waits in a bank (up to six hours) and is used on the next project you start.

    Away earnings

    While you are away (for at least five minutes), the bell gathers Bell-metal based on your best wave. It is about a tenth of what a full run to your best wave would melt into, for every hour you are away, for up to four hours. Each level of A patient bell adds one more hour to that cap. A welcome-back card shows what was gathered.

    Tip Start a long research project before bed and a short one before a walk.

    Daily tasks

    Each day brings three small tasks, such as "Reach wave 12 in one run", "Build five towers", "Call three waves early" or "Ring the bell back once". They scale with your best wave. Finishing them pays Bell-metal, a rewind charge for the next run or research time, and there is a daily chest as well. You get one free reroll a day, and a task that has become impossible can always be rerolled.

    See also Recast, Foundry and stars and Trials.
