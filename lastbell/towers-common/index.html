    Common towers

    The six common towers of The Last Bell: what each does, what it costs, its final forms and what it pairs with.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. Clapper
    2. Spring-gun
    3. Chime post
    4. Weight-drop
    5. Tack-thrower
    6. Bellows
    7. Which commons to start with?

    Commons make up most of every run. You can build as many as you have pads, and permanent stars make them strike harder in every later run.

    Tower Family Cost (Parts) Damage Strikes per second Reach (tiles) Opens at best wave
    Clapper Bell 20 6 1.0 2.2 start
    Spring-gun Spring 35 2.2 3.5 2.6 start
    Chime post Bell 50 4 0.8 2.0 start
    Weight-drop Weight 45 22 0.35 1.8 8
    Tack-thrower Spring 40 4 0.4 2.0 14
    Bellows Air 55 1 0.4 1.6 20

    The figures are for a level 1 tower, before upgrades and stars.

    Clapper

    Steady single strikes. A Bell-family tower that hits fliers as well as ground wind-ups, and the usual first tower.

    • Great Clapper: heavier strikes that stun the wind-up for a moment.
    • Twin Peal: strikes two wind-ups at once.

    Spring-gun

    Fast, light shots. The best tower for finishing weak, fast wind-ups like Scuttlers, and it can hit fliers.

    • Repeater: fires twice as fast.
    • Harpoon: each bolt pierces up to three wind-ups in a line. Very good on a long straight road.

    Chime post

    A small splash around its target. Good against clumps and against Clusters, and it hits fliers.

    • Wind Chimes: a much wider splash.
    • Resonator: wind-ups it strikes ring for 3 seconds, and every tower hits them 25 percent harder.

    Weight-drop

    Slow, crushing blows that shrug off armour. This is your answer to Platebacks. It cannot reach fliers.

    • Anvil: more than twice the damage, a little slower.
    • Tremor: shakes the ground, with a splash that stops wind-ups for a moment.

    Weight towers hit a wind-up harder if a Magnet coil has pulled its plates off. See Tower pairings.

    Tack-thrower

    Scatters tacks on the road; wind-ups walking over them are hurt. It works on the ground only, so it cannot reach fliers.

    • Caltrops: tacks last twice as long and slow whatever walks on them.
    • Brass Shrapnel: twice the damage over a wider spread.

    Bellows

    Puffs everything nearby back down the road. It nudges wind-ups backwards so your other towers have more time on them.

    • Gale: pushes twice as far.
    • Organ Pipe: blasts fliers, with four times the damage and a longer push against Gliders.

    Which commons to start with?

    A Clapper or Spring-gun on each road is a fine start. Add a Chime post where wind-ups bunch, then a Weight-drop once it opens at wave 8, so you have one ready when Platebacks arrive at wave 20. Next: Uncommon towers.

    Tip The Tack-thrower and Bellows do little alone, but they make every other tower near them more useful because wind-ups spend longer in reach.
