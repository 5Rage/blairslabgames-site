    Trials

    Challenge runs under harder rules, with goal waves, three stars each and early unlocks for epic towers.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    Spoilers ahead.This page discusses story events and reveals. Come back after you have played if you want to find things out yourself.
    On this page
    1. The seven classic Trials
    2. The handicap Trials
    3. Strategy

    A Trial is a run under a special rule, with a goal wave. Reach it once and you are paid Bell-metal; some Trials also unlock an epic tower early. Trials open after your third recast.

    Each Trial also has three stars: the goal wave is the first, 12 more waves is the second and 30 more the third. Later stars pay a smaller reward, once. You can retry a Trial as often as you like. The Carillon cannot be used in a Trial.

    The seven classic Trials

    Trial Rule Goal wave Bell-metal Also unlocks
    Common Stock Only common towers may be built 35 400 Weathercock
    Fog Day Every tower reaches 40 percent less far 40 500 Hourglass
    No Hindsight The bell cannot be rung back at all 40 800
    Lean Valley Only six pads to build on 40 600 Foundry crane
    Iron Hour Every wind-up wears plates all day 45 700 Pendulum
    The Long Night Wind-ups are a quarter tougher and walk faster 50 1000
    Short Day Midnight comes at wave 60: the Toll arrives in half the time 61 900 Orrery

    Finishing all seven unlocks the First Bell (see Legendary, Mythic and Exalted towers, spoilers).

    The handicap Trials

    Eight more Trials follow. Each opens only once you have reached a certain best wave and number of recasts, and pays Bell-metal and research time instead of a tower.

    Trial Rule Goal wave Bell-metal Opens at
    Bell Choir Only Bell towers 40 700 best wave 45, 4 recasts
    Spring Works Only Spring towers 40 700 best wave 50, 4 recasts
    Pea Soup Towers reach half as far, but every mend gives half as much again 40 750 best wave 50, 4 recasts
    Quick March Wind-ups march a third faster 45 800 best wave 55, 5 recasts
    Lean Parts A quarter of the starting Parts, and a quarter less from every mend 40 700 best wave 45, 4 recasts
    Slow Bell One rewind charge, and a charge takes three times as many mends to return 45 900 best wave 60, 6 recasts
    Dear Timber Towers cost 40 percent more to build and to upgrade 45 800 best wave 55, 5 recasts
    Brittle Spring The mainspring holds half as much 45 900 best wave 60, 6 recasts

    The first clear of a handicap Trial also gives 15 minutes of research time; a second star gives 20 minutes and a third star 10 minutes plus a rewind charge for your next run.

    Strategy

    • Common Stock is the easiest to start with, since you already own everything it allows.
    • Fog Day and Pea Soup: put your towers closer to the road and rely on splash and slows, since reach is short.
    • Lean Valley: choose your six pads for the corners where several road stretches meet.
    • No Hindsight: there is no safety net, so play carefully and build plenty early.
    • Bell Choir and Spring Works are good for learning how a family works together. See Tower pairings.
    • Slow Bell: you only get one charge, so save it.

    Tip The star targets are a good test for a new build. If it gets 12 waves past the goal, it's worth keeping.

    See also Daily Valley and the Peal.
