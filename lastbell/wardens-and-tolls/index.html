    Wardens and Tolls

    The mini-bosses that strike each hour and the four great Tolls at midnight, with their tricks and how to stop them.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    Spoilers ahead.This page discusses story events and reveals. Come back after you have played if you want to find things out yourself.
    On this page
    1. Wardens
    2. The Tolls
      1. The Toymaker's Drummer (wave 120)
      2. The Miller's Grindstone (wave 240)

    Warning This page names the Tolls and their stories. Skip it if you would like to meet them cold.

    Wardens

    Tamsin's notebook says the Wardens were the valley's finest work: one stood at every workshop gate and struck the hour as it passed. When the Rust came, they walked with the rest.

    In the game a Warden arrives as the last wind-up of every tenth wave, as the hour strikes. It is a large version of the newest kind of wind-up on the ground: at wave 10 a Scuttler, at 20 a Plateback, and so on up to a Conductor from wave 90. It is bigger, slower, heavier on the bell and far tougher than the others, and it pays much more.

    A Warden is also far more likely to wake and join you: roughly fifteen times as likely as an ordinary wind-up of its kind. See Crew and the Roll.

    The Tolls

    A Toll comes at midnight every 120 waves. Four Tolls come in turn and then the cycle repeats. A Toll can never wake as crew. Each pays a very large amount of Parts and, when you recast, a big share of Bell-metal.

    The Toymaker's Drummer (wave 120)

    Led the valley's parade every midsummer, from the toymaker's door to the bell. Its drumbeat makes every wind-up on the field walk faster (by a quarter) for as long as it stands, so stop it early. It is slow and armoured.

    The Miller's Grindstone (wave 240)

    The mill's great stone rolled out of the door and down the road. It rolls in bursts, running much faster for a moment, and sheds a ring of four Platebacks at half strength. Bring something that handles armour, such as the Weight-drop or Magnet coil.

    The Postmaster (wave 360)

    Never stopped trying to deliver the valley's letters. It posts flocks of Gliders as it walks, and a great many at half strength. Have fliers covered: a Weathercock and Bell towers near the bell.

    The Founder's Golem (wave 480)

    Tamsin's own helper, cast in her moulds. At half strength it recasts itself: for about 3 seconds it is molten and untouchable, and then whole again. Don't waste your big strikes while it is molten. Once the Golem is mended, you reach Tamsin's foundry.

    Tips

    • All Tolls are slow, well under half a Ticker's pace, so slows and holds count for more than raw damage.
    • Against the Drummer and Golem, open with your heaviest hits. Against the Grindstone and Postmaster, cover what they spawn.
    • The Short Day Trial brings the Drummer forward to wave 60.

    See also The clock face, Tips for deep waves and The story.
