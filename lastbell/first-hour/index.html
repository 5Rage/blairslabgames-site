    Your first hour

    What to build, what to expect and what unlocks during your first runs of The Last Bell.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. The guided first run
    2. What to expect
    3. A good opening
    4. After the first run

    The guided first run

    The very first run holds the first wave back until you have built a tower, and the lantern moth talks you through it:

    1. Tap a glowing stone pad beside the road.
    2. Choose the Clapper. It costs 20 Parts and strikes steadily.
    3. Mend a few wind-ups, then build a second tower on another pad.
    4. Tap a tower and choose the + to raise its level. Stronger towers hold later waves.
    5. You have one rewind charge, so save it. When a wind-up reaches the bell and the mainspring drops, tap Ring back 30 s.
    6. When the bell finally falls, recast it in the Foundry to grow stronger.

    What to expect

    You begin with three towers: the Clapper, the Spring-gun and the Chime post. The rest open as your best wave grows (details on Common towers):

    Best wave reached New tower
    8 Weight-drop
    14 Tack-thrower
    16 Kennel
    20 Bellows
    25 Damper
    30 Catchwheel

    Your first Warden arrives at wave 10, a larger wind-up of the newest kind on the road. The wind-ups themselves come in this order: Tickers from the first wave, Scuttlers from wave 10, Platebacks from wave 20 and Gliders from wave 31. See The wind-ups.

    A good opening

    • Two towers early beat one strong tower. Cover the road in two places before you upgrade.
    • Mix a fast tower with a heavy one. The Spring-gun's quick light shots and the Weight-drop's slow crushing blows cover each other's gaps.
    • Look at the strip under the field. A new kind of wind-up is marked "new" there, so you can plan for it.
    • Spend as you go. Unspent Parts are lost when the run ends.

    After the first run

    When the mainspring runs down, recast. Open the Foundry and buy the cheap upgrades first. Spare Parts and Stronger mainspring help straight away, and More pads lets you build more. Then start another run. Each one should reach a little further, and after your third recast the Trials open.

    Towers unlock by the best wave you have ever reached, in any run, so even a run that ends badly can open something new.

    Tip The guided run suggests ringing back when a wind-up first reaches the bell. Do it, because the echoes show you how to read a failing wave.
