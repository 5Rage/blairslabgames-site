    The wind-ups

    Every kind of clockwork wind-up in The Last Bell, when it first appears, how it behaves and what beats it.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page

    2. Ticker
    3. Scuttler
    4. Plateback

    7. Cluster

    11. Mixed waves

    Wind-ups are old toys and tools that ran down and rusted. Your towers mend them rather than destroy them. Each kind has its own silhouette, one visible trait and one obvious counter, so you can read a wave from the strip under the field.

    The roster

    Wind-up First appears Base health Speed Harm to the bell Parts when mended
    Ticker wave 1 10 0.9 4 3
    Scuttler wave 10 6 1.7 3 3
    Plateback wave 20 30 0.6 8 8
    Glider wave 31 9 1.1 5 5
    Spinner wave 41 12 0.8 5 5
    Cluster wave 51 14 0.8 6 3
    Oiler wave 61 16 0.75 4 6
    Brollyback wave 71 20 0.7 7 7
    Conductor wave 81 18 0.85 5 7

    These are the values at wave 1. Health, Parts and harm all grow with each wave: health by about 11.5 percent a wave up to wave 50, then by 7 percent a wave.

    Ticker

    The basic walker, round and rusty. You will mend thousands of them.

    Scuttler

    Fast and fragile. A mechanical beetle. Quick towers like the Spring-gun are ideal, and a Bellows or a Damper buys time.

    Plateback

    Slow and armoured. A clockwork tortoise. Its casing takes a little off every strike, which punishes fast, weak shots. Answer with the Weight-drop, which shrugs off armour, or pull the plates off with a Magnet coil.

    Glider

    Fliers on paper wings. Gliders ignore the roads and fly straight at the bell, so some of your towers must stand near it. Only Spring, Bell and Air towers can strike them (check each tower's card). They cannot be slowed by oil or a Damper, and a Catchwheel cannot drag them. The Weathercock is the specialist.

    Spinner

    A spinning-top with a crossbow. It stops at range and throws bolts at your towers. A bolt jams the nearest tower for 1.5 seconds, every 4 seconds. Kill it quickly, or keep strong towers out of its range.

    Cluster

    Three Tickers clumped together. Stop it and it comes apart into all three. Splash towers like the Chime post make quick work of the pieces.

    Oiler

    A walking oil can that oils the wind-ups around it back to health (about 4 percent of their health every second). Pick it off first.

    Brollyback

    A soldier with an umbrella. The umbrella soaks up half its health in strikes and opens again if it is left alone for 4 seconds. Steady fire keeps it folded, and the Sounding horn's blast folds it immediately.

    Conductor

    A conductor with a baton who winds up the wind-ups around him so they walk 30 percent faster. Kill him first.

    Mixed waves

    From wave 31 onwards, waves mix kinds, and more join every ten waves. The 41st wave, for instance, adds Spinners to a wave already holding Tickers, Scuttlers, Platebacks and Gliders. The wave preview under the field tells you what is coming and marks each new kind.

    Every hour on the clock a Warden comes, a large one of the newest kind of wind-up, and at midnight the Toll comes. See The clock face for the schedule and Tower pairings for answers.

    Tip The mended sometimes wake and join you. See Crew and the Roll.
