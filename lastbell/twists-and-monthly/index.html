    Twists and the monthly valley

    Every twist that can change a valley, and how the monthly valley rotates.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. Every twist
    2. The monthly valley
    3. How to approach each twist

    A twist is a rule that changes a whole run. Some make it harder, some easier, and some do both. The monthly valley, the Daily Valley and the Peal all use twists.

    Every twist

    Twist What it does
    Fog Towers see 20 percent less far, but every mend gives twice the Parts
    Harvest Gliders arrive from wave 12 instead of 31
    Brass Start with three times the Parts; wind-ups are 15 percent tougher
    Rust The mainspring holds 25 percent less, but the run is worth 50 percent more Bell-metal
    Iron Hour Every wind-up wears armour plates
    Swift Roads Wind-ups walk 15 percent faster, but give 25 percent more Parts
    Lean Valley Only six pads to build on, whatever the Foundry says
    Cheap Timber Towers cost 30 percent less to build and 30 percent more to upgrade
    Short Memory Ringing back the minute gives no Hindsight

    The monthly valley

    Each calendar month, one hand-made valley is featured with one of four twists (Fog, Harvest, Brass or Rust), the same for every player. Past months stay playable.

    The featured valley is chosen among the first five hand-made valleys, so it is always one you will already have unlocked: Crossroads, Switchback Hill, the Spiral, the Mill Race and Orchard Rows. See Hand-made valleys.

    How to approach each twist

    • Fog. Place towers right beside the road. Splash and slows matter more, and Parts come quickly, so build wide.
    • Harvest. Gliders turn up from wave 12. Build a Spring-gun, Gong or Bell tower near the bell by wave 10.
    • Brass. You start with a big purse, so spend it on a strong set-up quickly, then keep upgrading.
    • Rust. The mainspring is smaller. Rewind charges and a Mender's post help, and the payoff is extra Bell-metal.
    • Iron Hour. Armour means Weight-drops and Magnet coils.
    • Swift Roads. Slow them with a Damper, Oilcan or Kennel.
    • Lean Valley. Choose your six pads carefully.
    • Cheap Timber. Build lots and upgrade little.
    • Short Memory. Each charge is a single use, as there is no stacking bonus.

    See Valleys, Daily Valley and the Peal and Trials.
