    Recast, the Foundry and stars

    How a run turns into Bell-metal and how to spend it on permanent upgrades and tower stars.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. Recast
    2. The Foundry
      1. What to buy first
    3. Stars
    4. Unlocks

    Recast

    A run ends when the bell tower's mainspring runs down. From wave 20 you may also recast by choice from the run screen. Either way, the run melts into Bell-metal: the further you got, the more you receive, and Wardens and Tolls stopped add a good bonus on top. The number is shown on the button ("Recast now +278") before you commit.

    Kept Cleared
    Bell-metal, tower unlocks, stars, Foundry upgrades, research, the Roll of woken wind-ups, story Parts, towers on the field, the wave count, this run's crew

    The best wave you reach also counts for good: it is what opens new towers, valleys and Trials.

    Tip If you've stalled, recast by choice from wave 20. A fresh run reaches the same waves faster with the Bell-metal you just gained.

    The Foundry

    Bell-metal buys permanent upgrades. Every price rises each time you buy that upgrade.

    Upgrade What it does Starting price Levels
    More pads One more stone pad to build on in every run 30 8
    Spare Parts Start each run with 20 more Parts 12 15
    Stronger mainspring The mainspring holds 15 percent more 18 12
    Truer strikes Every tower strikes 15 percent harder 20 30
    Scavenging 10 percent more Parts from every mend 20 20
    The bell remembers The bell can hold one more rewind charge 140 2
    Quick bell A rewind charge comes back 5 mends sooner 45 5
    Crew quarters Start each run with one more crew from the Roll 300 3
    The bell keeps watch When the mainspring would run down, the bell rings back by itself if it has a charge 250 1
    A second bench Research two projects at once 400 1

    Prices climb by a fixed share with each level: from about 38 percent more per level for Truer strikes, to 75 percent for More pads, and four times for The bell remembers.

    What to buy first

    1. Spare Parts and Stronger mainspring are cheap and help from the first wave.
    2. More pads gives you somewhere to build; you begin with eight.
    3. Truer strikes is the long-term workhorse: 30 levels of 15 percent.
    4. The bell remembers and The bell keeps watch make the Rewind much more forgiving.
    5. A second bench doubles your research.

    Stars

    Each tower you have unlocked can be given stars in the Foundry. A star makes that tower strike 25 percent harder in every later run (compounding), up to 15 stars. The first star costs 25 Bell-metal for a Common tower, and 40, 65, 100, 150, 225 or 300 for Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic or Exalted. Each further star costs about 45 percent more than the last.

    Stars go on the tower itself, so both final forms benefit. Put them on the towers you actually build.

    Unlocks

    New towers and valleys open themselves as your best wave grows; the home screen shows the next one. See Towers and Valleys. At three recasts the Trials open.

    Note The Open Field, the Daily Valley and the Peal each have their own separate starting point, so their upgrades do not carry over to your main valley (and vice versa). See The Open Field and Daily Valley and the Peal.
