    The clock face

    How the bell tower's clock marks a run: an hour every ten waves, a Warden at each hour and the Toll at midnight.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. The rhythm
    2. New things on the way
    3. Wardens
    4. The Toll
    5. Why it matters

    The clock face on the bell tower tracks your run. The top of the screen shows where you are, with something like "3 o'clock, strikes at wave 41".

    The rhythm

    • A wave arrives about every 30 seconds, or sooner if you call it early.
    • Every 10 waves the hand moves one hour. The day starts at 12 o'clock.
    • When an hour strikes, a Warden arrives, a mini-boss that is a large one of the newest kind of wind-up on the ground.
    • At midnight, wave 120, the Toll comes, a boss. Then the day starts again, and the next Toll comes at wave 240, then 360 and so on.
    Wave What comes
    10, 20, 30, ... A Warden
    120 The Toymaker's Drummer
    240 The Miller's Grindstone
    360 The Postmaster
    480 The Founder's Golem
    600 onward The four Tolls come round again in the same order

    Waves 120, 240 and so on bring a Toll instead of a Warden.

    New things on the way

    The first time each kind of wind-up appears, the wave preview marks it "new". Tickers begin at wave 1, Scuttlers at 10, Platebacks at 20, Gliders at 31, and one new kind each hour after that until the Conductors at wave 81. See The wind-ups.

    Wardens

    Each Warden drops a lot of Parts, and there is a chance, far higher than for ordinary wind-ups, that it wakes and joins you as crew. Wardens count towards the Bell-metal you earn when you recast. More on Wardens and Tolls.

    The Toll

    A Toll is a huge bell-ringer from one of the valley's old workshops. Stopping one pays a lot of Bell-metal and moves the story on. Each Toll has its own tricks; see Wardens and Tolls (spoilers) and The story.

    Why it matters

    • Planning: a Warden is due every tenth wave, so spend your Parts before it arrives.
    • Twists: some valleys and twists change reach, speed or Parts for the whole run.
    • Short Day: one of the Trials brings midnight forward to wave 60.

    Tip Check the top bar before a big wave. If the hand is about to strike, a Warden is the last wind-up of the wave.
