    Legendary, Mythic and Exalted towers

    Tamsin Hale's own designs and the crown of the collection: the Carillon, the Still Point, the Furnace and beyond.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    Spoilers ahead.This page discusses story events and reveals. Come back after you have played if you want to find things out yourself.
    On this page
    1. Legendary
    2. Mythic
    3. Exalted
    4. How to use them

    Warning Late-game spoilers. These towers open at waves far beyond a first run, and their names come from the notebook's later pages.

    Only one tower of each rarity (Legendary, Mythic, Exalted) may stand on the field at a time, so pick the one you want to build around.

    Legendary

    The Carillon. Every strike chimes on to the next wind-up and the next, fliers too, and its chime makes every tower in reach strike harder. It opens when you reach wave 361, the third Toll. Not allowed in Trials. - Grand Carillon: its chime leaps on twice as many times. - Tower Bells: towers in its reach strike twice as much harder.

    The Still Point. Every few seconds it stops time in its circle: wind-ups on the ground there freeze while the other towers strike. - Stillness: time stays stopped much longer. - Wide Calm: stops time in a circle half as wide again.

    Tamsin's Furnace. Molten bronze pools where it strikes and burns what walks in it. Oiled wind-ups burn twice as fast, and wind-ups mended in it give double Parts. - Crucible: its pools burn twice as hot. - Bellows-fed: wider pools that last longer.

    The Echo Spire. Every strike happens twice: five seconds later its echo strikes the same wind-up again. - Long Echo: its echoes strike half as hard again. - Twin Spire: its echoes come back twice as soon.

    The Second Hand. Every few seconds it sends the toughest wind-up in reach back to the very start of its road. A Toll is sent back a third of the way. - Sweep Hand: sends two wind-ups back at once. - Hour Hand: turns back time more often.

    Mythic

    The Grand Escapement. Strikes hard, and every twenty seconds every wind-up on the field stops for a heartbeat. - Deadbeat: stops the whole field more often. - Remontoire: stops the whole field twice as long.

    The Long Pendulum. A pendulum as tall as the valley sweeps every wind-up on the ground, anywhere on the field, every ten seconds. - Foucault: sweeps the field more often. - Lead Bob: sweeps twice as hard and stops what it hits.

    The Starwheel. A wheel of brass stars turns over the whole field, each star striking a wind-up on the ground. It burns brightest at midnight. - Constellation: ten stars instead of six. - Midnight Wheel: blazes twice as bright whenever a Warden or a Toll walks.

    Exalted

    The First Bell. Tamsin Hale's first bell. Each time it rings, every tower strikes as if the minute had been rung back, and wind-ups in its reach are wound down to a crawl. It opens when you have finished every one of the seven classic Trials, or by reaching wave 1441. - Tamsin's Peal: its ring counts as ringing the minute back twice. - The Valley's Voice: its crawl reaches twice as far.

    How to use them

    Time-family legendaries multiply what the rest of your field already does, by freezing wind-ups so the others can strike. The Still Point wants a stretch of road with strikers around it, and the Echo Spire wants a tower with big single strikes to repeat. Stars are the best investment here, since they are permanent and compound. See Recast, Foundry and stars and Tips for deep waves.
