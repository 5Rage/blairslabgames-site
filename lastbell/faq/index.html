    Frequently asked questions

    Quick answers about rewinding, recasting, unlocks, time away, Trials and more in The Last Bell.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page

    2. Rewind
    3. Progress

    Basics

    What is The Last Bell? A hand-painted tower-defence game. You build towers beside the roads to stop clockwork wind-ups reaching the last bell tower, and you can ring the bell backwards to replay the last minute. See How to play.

    Do I need to sign in to anything? No. Signing in to Google Play Games is optional and play never depends on it. The Peal compares scores with a group of other keepers.

    Which languages does it have? Twelve.

    Is my run saved? Yes: your run is saved exactly where you left it.

    Rewind

    How many times can I rewind? You start with one charge. A charge comes back after 40 mends. The Foundry can raise the most the bell can hold to three, and speed up the recharge. See Rewind.

    How far back does it go? Thirty seconds.

    Does the same thing happen when I replay? Yes: the wind-ups come in the same order, the same way. What you changed is what makes the difference.

    What is Hindsight? Your towers strike 10 percent harder each time you replay a stretch (up to 30 percent).

    Progress

    What is Bell-metal? What a run melts into when you recast. It buys Foundry upgrades, tower stars and research. See Recast, Foundry and stars.

    What do I lose when I recast? Parts, the towers on the field, the wave count and the run's crew. You keep Bell-metal, unlocks, stars, the Foundry, research and the Roll.

    How do I unlock towers? By your best wave, in any run. Some Trials open an epic early. See Towers.

    How do I unlock valleys? Hand-made valleys open at best waves 15, 25, 35, and so on up to 95. See The valleys.

    When can I recast? When the run ends, or by choice from wave 20.

    Where did my Parts go? Parts only live during a run. Spend them as you go.

    Towers

    Why can't some towers hit Gliders? Weight and Oil towers cannot strike fliers; Spring, Bell and Air towers generally can. Each tower's card says so.

    What are final forms? At level 10 each tower chooses one of two final forms. Both are listed on the tower pages.

    Why can't I build another rare tower? Only four Rare, two Epic, and one each of Legendary, Mythic and Exalted towers may stand on the field at once.

    What do stars do? Each star makes a tower strike 25 percent harder in every later run, up to 15.

    Away from the game

    What happens if I leave mid-run? The tower holds. The wave does not move, so you do not come back to a lost run. See Research and time away.

    Does research keep running? Yes, even when the game is closed.

    Challenges

    What are Trials? Challenge runs with special rules. They open after three recasts. See Trials.

    What is the Daily Valley? A small seeded run that is the same for everyone on a given day. See Daily Valley and the Peal.

    What is the Peal? A fortnightly competition: a separate world, groups of 100, best wave wins, no chat.

    Does the Peal or the Daily Valley affect my main game? Only through small Bell-metal thank-yous. Their own upgrades stay in their own worlds.

    The world

    Who is Tamsin Hale? The bell-founder who kept the bell tower before you, and left you her notebook. See The story (spoilers).

    What does the moth do? It settles by one tower at a time, and that tower strikes truer. See The lantern moth.
