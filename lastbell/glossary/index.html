    Glossary

    The words of the valley, from Bell-metal and Hindsight to the Toll and the Roll.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    Term Meaning
    Bell-metal What a recast melts a run into. Buys Foundry upgrades, tower stars and research. Kept for good.
    Bell tower The tower in the middle of the field. If its mainspring runs down, the run ends.
    Brass, Fog, Harvest, Rust Four twists that change a whole valley.
    Call early Bringing the next wave immediately, for bonus Parts.
    Casing A wind-up's armour plates; each strike loses a little to it.
    Charge One use of the bell's rewind. See Rewind.
    Clock face The bell tower's clock, marking the hour every ten waves. See The clock face.
    Crew A woken wind-up placed in a tower, adding its strength. See Crew and the Roll.
    Daily Valley The seeded run that is the same for everyone each day.
    Echoes The faint trails left by an erased minute after a rewind.
    Family Bell, Spring, Weight, Air, Light, Oil or Time: the group a tower belongs to.
    Final form The two choices at tower level 10.
    Foundry Where Bell-metal is spent on upgrades, stars and research.
    Hindsight The strike bonus from replaying a minute: 10 percent each time, up to 30.
    Keeper You.
    Mainspring The bell tower's health.
    Mend What happens when a wind-up is stopped: the Rust falls away and it drops Parts.
    Moth The lantern moth that settles by one tower at a time. See The lantern moth.
    Open Field The roadless maze valley. See The Open Field.
    Pads The stone plots you build towers on.
    Parts What mended wind-ups drop; spent in a run, not kept.
    Peal The fortnightly competition.
    Recast Ending a run to turn it into Bell-metal.
    Roll The record of every wind-up that has woken and joined.
    Rust What has got into the wind-ups' springs.
    Stars Permanent levels for each tower, bought with Bell-metal.
    Tamsin Hale The bell-founder who kept the tower before you.
    Toll The great boss at midnight. See Wardens and Tolls.
    Trial A challenge run with special rules. See Trials.
    Twist A rule that changes a run, such as Fog.
    Warden The mini-boss that arrives each hour.
    Wind-up A clockwork foe. See The wind-ups.
