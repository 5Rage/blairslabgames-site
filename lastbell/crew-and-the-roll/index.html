    Crew and the Roll

    How mended wind-ups sometimes wake and join your defence, and how the Roll remembers them.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. Waking
    2. Crew
    3. The Roll
    4. Tips

    Most mended wind-ups lie down and stay down. Occasionally one wakes up and joins you as crew. Tamsin wrote that some of them always would: "Wind them with kindness and a few will wind back."

    Waking

    Every mend has a small chance of waking the wind-up: about one in 400 for the plainest kinds, a little better for tougher ones, and about fifteen times as likely for a Warden. A Toll never wakes. Waking has its own luck, so it never changes the roads or the waves.

    Crew

    A woken wind-up becomes crew. You place it in a tower (one crew member per tower), and it adds its own strength:

    Wind-up What its crew adds
    Ticker +15 percent strikes
    Scuttler +25 percent speed
    Plateback +25 percent strikes
    Glider +20 percent reach
    Spinner +20 percent speed, +10 percent strikes
    Cluster +20 percent strikes
    Oiler +15 percent speed, +15 percent strikes
    Brollyback +30 percent strikes
    Conductor +35 percent speed
    Any Warden extra +25 percent strikes and +20 percent speed on top

    Crew serve for the run that woke them.

    The Roll

    The first time a kind wakes, it is written on the Roll for good. The Roll is kept through every recast, and each later waking of the same kind makes that crew stronger: its bonus grows by a tenth for every waking after the first, up to double.

    The Foundry's Crew quarters upgrade lets you start each run with crew from the Roll, the strongest first (Wardens first, then the latest kinds), up to three.

    Tips

    • Put speed-crew (Scuttler, Conductor) in fast towers and strike-crew (Brollyback, Plateback) in heavy hitters.
    • A Warden's crew is the best, so every Warden you stop is a small chance at a strong helper.
    • The Roll lists every kind of wind-up that has woken so far, if you want to check what you're missing.

    See The wind-ups, Wardens and Tolls and Recast, Foundry and stars.
