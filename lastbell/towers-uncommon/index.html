    Uncommon towers

    Eight uncommon towers that slow, hold, hook, oil, speed up and ring out, with their costs, unlock waves and final forms.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. Kennel
    2. Damper
    3. Catchwheel
    4. Cog-saw
    5. Oilcan
    6. Lantern post
    7. Gong
    8. Key-winder
    9. Picking uncommons

    Uncommon towers slow, hold, drag, slick and amplify. They open between waves 16 and 60.

    Tower Family Cost Damage Strikes per second Reach Opens at best wave

    Damper Weight 60 none n/a 1.8 25

    Kennel

    Sends a clockwork hound to hold the leading wind-up in place while other towers strike. It cannot reach fliers.

    • Pack: two hounds at once, holding two wind-ups.
    • Mastiff: holds twice as long, and Wardens and Tolls cannot shake it off sooner.

    Damper

    Everything near it walks slower. It cannot slow fliers.

    • Deep Damper: slows a fifth more.
    • Rust-eater: wind-ups in its mist take 25 percent more from every tower.

    Catchwheel

    Hooks the leading wind-up and drags it back along the road. It cannot hook fliers.

    • Long Line: drags twice as far.
    • Snare Wheel: hooks two wind-ups at once.

    Cog-saw

    A spinning cog that cuts through several wind-ups in a line. It works best on a straight stretch of road.

    • Buzz-saw: spins faster and cuts through five more.
    • Great Cog: twice the damage along a wider line.

    Oilcan

    Slicks the road: wind-ups on it slip, walk slower and take more from every strike. It cannot reach fliers.

    • Wide Slick: slicks a wider stretch of road, for longer.
    • Lamp Oil: the slick burns, and whatever walks on it is hurt.

    Lantern post

    Its light lets the towers near it reach further and strike harder. It is a support and strikes nothing itself. The moth likes Light towers.

    • Beacon: its light reaches half as far again.
    • Searchlight: towers in its light strike twice as hard as before.

    Gong

    Every few seconds a ring of sound hits everything around it, fliers too.

    • Temple Gong: rings twice as hard.
    • Thunder Gong: its ring stops everything it hits for a moment.

    Key-winder

    Winds the towers near it so they strike faster. It is a support tower with no attack of its own.

    • Mainspring: winds the towers near it twice as tight.
    • Twin Keys: reaches towers twice as far away.

    Picking uncommons

    • Use a Kennel or Catchwheel where one road is leaking. Both give your other towers more seconds.
    • Put a Damper or Oilcan at the start of a long road, with strikers beyond it.
    • Put a Lantern post or Key-winder in the middle of a tight cluster of towers, since every tower within reach gets the effect.
