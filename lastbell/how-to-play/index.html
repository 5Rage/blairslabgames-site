    How to play

    The whole loop of The Last Bell in one page: build, defend, rewind, recast, improve.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. The screen
    2. Build
    3. Defend
    4. Rewind
    5. Recast
    6. Improve for good

    The screen

    The field is a square with the bell tower in the middle, sized for a portrait phone. Roads run in from the edges to the bell. Along the top you see the wave number, the hour on the clock face, the mainspring (the bell tower's health) and your Parts. Beneath the field a strip shows the next wave: its wind-ups as small pictures with counts, a "new" mark the first time a kind appears, and the seconds until it arrives. At the bottom are Ring back 30 s, a speed button and Pause.

    Build

    Tap a glowing stone pad to choose a tower from the ring. Pads sit beside the roads and around the tower itself. You begin with eight pads, and the Foundry can add more. Each tower costs Parts. The Clapper, the usual first tower, costs 20, and you start a run with 45.

    Tap a tower you have built to upgrade it (a tower can be raised to level 10 within a run) or sell it. Each level makes a tower strike harder, but the next level costs more: each upgrade costs a little over one and a half times the one before. Selling returns 70 percent of what you spent. After a rewind, while the field is paused, selling returns everything.

    Defend

    A wave arrives about every 30 seconds, and the waves grow tougher. Wind-ups walk the road to the bell. Every one that reaches it takes some of the mainspring, though the bell tower strikes back weakly, so a few strays are never fatal. Every wind-up your towers stop is mended, and drops Parts.

    Call the next wave early. Once the current wave has finished arriving, a "Call now" button offers to bring the next wave immediately for bonus Parts: the more time you skip, the bigger the bonus. Calling early earns more, but there's no penalty for letting it play itself.

    Rewind

    You start with one rewind charge. When a wave goes wrong, tap Ring back 30 s. The whole story is on the Rewind page.

    Recast

    When the mainspring runs down, the run is over. You can also recast by choice from wave 20 onwards. Either way the run melts into Bell-metal: the further you got, and the more Wardens and Tolls you stopped, the more you receive.

    Parts, the towers on the field and the wave count are cleared. Bell-metal, tower unlocks, Foundry upgrades, research and the Roll of woken wind-ups are kept.

    Improve for good

    Spend Bell-metal in the Foundry on permanent upgrades and on tower stars, and start research. New towers and valleys open on their own as you reach further waves.

    Tip Build a second tower before you upgrade the first, save your rewind charge for a wave that surprises you, and keep an eye on the next-wave strip.
