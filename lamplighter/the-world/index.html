    The lamplighter and the world

    The valley of stopped clocks, the roads that need lighting, and the moth that follows your lantern.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. A world that stopped
    2. The lamplighters
    3. The lantern moth
    4. The roads

    Note The game is in testing, so details may change.

    A world that stopped

    Every clock in the valley has stopped. On the roads between the valley and a city that stands still in the rain, the Rust comes out after dark. These are broken, half-wound clockwork things from fairgrounds, mills, markets and railway cuttings. The lamps along those roads have to be lit again every night.

    The Lamplighter shares a world with the other BlairsLab games. You do not need any of them to play this one.

    The lamplighters

    You begin as Linnet, keeper of the Wayward Light and the first to walk the road. Other lamplighters can be earned by playing, each with a different first fitting and a small strength: a tinker, a bell-ringer, a quick runner, a lock-keeper from the canal, and a clockmaker from the city who came out to see why the lamps went dark. Their names and how to open them are on The tower and the lamplighters.

    The lantern moth

    A small moth follows your light along every road. Once a night, when you are about to fall, it flares. The nearby Rust is thrown back, you are safe for two seconds, and you get back up with some of your health restored. A tower upgrade called The moth stays lets it save you twice a night.

    The roads

    Fifteen roads run from the fairground at the valley's edge, through orchards, a stopped mill race, a market and a canal, over a long bridge hung with silent bells, and on to the city gate and the wall beyond. Each has its own scenery, its own weather in the air (confetti, leaves, mist, sparks, wind, rain hanging still, embers), its own lamps and its own boss. They are listed on Roads and bosses.
