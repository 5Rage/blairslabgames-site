    Note The Lamplighter is still in testing and has not had a wide release. These pages describe the game as testers see it today, and details may change.

    What it is

    Every clock in the valley has stopped, and after dark the Rust comes out: broken, half-wound clockwork things that crawl, flutter and fizz towards you. You walk the roads and keep the lamps lit.

    You play on a phone in short sessions. You drag a thumb anywhere on the screen to walk, and your lantern does the fighting on its own, with bolts of light, rings of sparks and a hand bell that knocks the crowd back. You never aim. Your job is to move well, pick up the glowing embers that mended Rust-things leave behind, and choose what to add to your lantern each time you level up.

    What makes it different

    The streets are lined with unlit lamps. Stand beside one for a moment and it lights. Rust inside a lit lamp's glow moves slower and takes more damage from everything you throw at it. Your lantern's oil slowly burns down, and a lit lamp is where you refill it, so you are always deciding which lamp to light next and when to leave its light.

    The shape of a night

    • A night lasts about five minutes, and you can pause at any time.
    • Level up often, and choose one of three fittings each time.
    • Puzzles, lost satchels and chests of light turn up along the road.
    • At the end of the night a boss arrives, with a red warning before every big attack.
    • Win or fall, you take wick-silver home to your tower for lasting upgrades.
