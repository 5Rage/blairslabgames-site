    How a night feels

    A walk through one run, from the first embers to the boss at the end of the road.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. Moving
    2. The first minute
    3. Lamps
    4. Things on the road
    5. When you are about to fall
    6. The boss

    Note The game is in testing, so details here may change.

    Moving

    You drag a thumb anywhere on the screen and the lamplighter walks that way. Your lantern strikes the nearest Rust-thing by itself, and any companions you collect do their own work. The skill is in where you walk, towards embers and lamps and away from the big slow ones.

    The top of the screen keeps three bars: your health, your lantern's oil, and your level. The countdown tells you how long until dawn, and a small count shows how many Rust-things you have mended so far.

    The first minute

    Things start gently. A few Rattlers and bits of Soot wander in from the dark, you mend them, and they drop embers. Walk over an ember to gather it. Fill the level bar and you stop for a moment: three fittings are offered and you pick one. Early picks matter, because a fitting can be raised five times and two of them can eventually fuse into something stronger. See Lights and fittings.

    Lamps

    Walk up to an unlit street lamp and stay beside it for a moment, and it lights. A lit lamp does three things:

    • It slows the Rust inside its glow by 30%.
    • Anything standing in the glow takes 25% more damage from you.
    • It refills your lantern's oil while you stand in it.

    Two lit lamps close together also join with a thread of light that burns whatever crosses between them. If your oil runs dry, the Rust bites 50% harder, so stay near the lamps.

    Things on the road

    • Lost satchels turn up now and then and wait about 45 seconds before they are gone: a hot pie, a lodestone, flash powder, a silver purse, an oil can. See the FAQ for what each does.
    • Puzzles appear twice in a night and last 75 seconds each: bells that ring a tune you have to ring back, plates that must all be held down at once, or a spark to carry to a cold brazier. Solving one leaves a chest of light and some wick-silver.
    • Elite Rust-things carry chests. Usually a chest holds one fitting, but now and then it holds three, and rarely five.

    When you are about to fall

    A small lantern moth follows your light. Once a night, when you would otherwise fall, it flares, throwing the nearby Rust back and giving you a couple of safe seconds to recover. One of the tower's upgrades lets it do this twice. See the world.

    The boss

    When the night's five minutes are up, the boss of the road arrives. Every road has its own, and each has a handful of attacks. Before any big attack there is a red warning on the ground, so the fight is mostly reading the ground and walking. Mend it and the night is won. After that, whether you win or fall, your wick-silver goes home with you. See Roads and bosses.
