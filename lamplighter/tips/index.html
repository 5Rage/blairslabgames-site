    Tips for your first nights

    Practical habits for lighting lamps, choosing fittings and getting the first boss down.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. On the road
    2. Building your lantern
    3. Bosses
    4. Between nights

    Note The game is in testing, so details here may change.

    On the road

    • Light the lamps early. Each one takes only a moment of standing beside it, and every lit lamp slows the Rust in its glow by 30% and makes them take 25% more damage. The first road has eight.
    • Watch your oil. When the lantern runs dry the Rust bites 50% harder. A lit lamp refills it as long as you stand in its glow, so work your route from lamp to lamp.
    • Use the lines. Two lit lamps within about ten tiles of each other are joined by a thread of light that burns what crosses it. If you can lead a crowd across one, you get free damage.
    • Keep moving. Your lantern aims itself, so never stop to shoot.
    • Grab the satchels. They vanish after about 45 seconds. A lodestone pulls every ember on the road to you, and a hot pie mends a third of your health.

    Building your lantern

    • Chase a fusion. Taking one weapon to level 5 and holding its partner fitting turns it into something much stronger. Lantern beam with Sharp lenses, Hand bell with Wider light and Tin hound with Thick coat are all natural pairs. See Lights and fittings.
    • Do not spread thin. You can carry five weapons, but a few well-raised ones beat many level-ones.
    • Passives help everything. Sharp lenses, Wider light and Winding key improve every weapon you have.
    • Reroll, banish or skip. If three choices are all wrong for you, take the silver for skipping. Banishing a fitting you will never want makes better ones turn up more often.
    • Open the chests. Elite chests give free picks, and a solved puzzle's chest always holds three.

    Bosses

    • Read the red. Every big attack warns you on the ground first. Move out of the red and return to attack.
    • Fight near light. Lit lamps near the boss's arena make it take 25% more damage while it stands in their glow.
    • Rain, Snuff and Spiral each need a different answer: step off the marked ground, relight lamps when Snuff puts them out, and slip between the arms of a Spiral.
    • Come back stronger. If a boss beats you, spend your silver in the tower and try again.

    Between nights

    • Put early silver into Sturdier keeper, Bigger tank and Keen edge: cheap, and they help every night.
    • Try your new lamplighters. Odo, Ismay, Corrie and Ambrose open after Night I of particular roads, and Tuck after any Night II. See The tower and the lamplighters.
    • If a road is too hard, a different lamplighter or a few polishes on your lantern parts can be enough.
