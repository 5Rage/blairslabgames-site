    The tower and the lamplighters

    How wick-silver, tower upgrades, lantern parts and new lamplighters make each night a little easier than the last.

    Updated 11 November 2026

    On this page
    1. Tower upgrades
    2. Lantern parts
    3. The lamplighters

    Note The game is in testing, so details here may change.

    Whether you win a night or fall, the wick-silver you earned goes home with you. It is what you spend on getting stronger between runs. There are no random packs and no energy: you can play as many nights as you like.

    Tower upgrades

    The tower sells lasting upgrades for wick-silver. Each one can be bought several times, and each purchase costs a bit more than the last.

    Upgrade What it does Times you can buy it
    Sturdier keeper 10% more health 50
    Brighter glass Everything reaches 5% further 10
    Bigger tank The lantern holds 10% more oil 10
    Light feet You walk 4% faster 8
    Long reach Embers come to you from 10% further 8
    Keen edge Everything strikes 6% harder 80
    Second thoughts One more reroll of a level's choices each night 3
    Clear mind One more banish each night 3
    The moth stays The moth can save you twice a night 1

    Lantern parts

    Every boss gives you a fixed lantern part the first time you win each of its three nights, so each road gives three parts and the fifteen roads give forty-five. A new part fills an empty slot by itself. There are three kinds of slot:

    • Glass adds strike damage and reach.
    • Wick adds oil and mends a little health every second.
    • Frame adds health.

    Parts from later roads are stronger. You can polish any part up to +10 with wick-silver, and each polish makes it better and the next one dearer.

    The lamplighters

    You start as Linnet. The others are earned by playing, never by chance. Each begins with a different first fitting and has a small strength.

    Lamplighter First fitting Strength How to open
    Linnet Lantern beam Lantern holds 15% more oil Ready from the start
    Odo Tin hound Companions strike 25% harder, 10% more health Win Night I of Orchard Lane
    Ismay Hand bell Everything reaches 15% further Win Night I of The Tinker's Market
    Tuck Spark ring Walks 10% faster, embers come from 25% further Win any Night II
    Corrie Oil trail 20% more health, mends 0.4 health a second Win Night I of The Railway Cutting
    Ambrose Clockwork crow Everything strikes 12% harder Win Night I of The Moor Road

    Pick your lamplighter on the Lantern tab, where your parts live too. Each lamplighter's first fitting changes how a night plays, so try a few (see Lights and fittings).

    The game also has an Almanac, a book of rewards you work through by playing (mending Rust, lighting lamps, beating bosses), and past pages stay claimable. For where to spend your first silver, see Tips for your first nights.
